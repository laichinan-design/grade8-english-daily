# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-11

**主題：A Family Picnic by the River（河邊野餐）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Every autumn, the Chen family has a picnic by the river near their town. This Saturday, Dad checked the weather report and said, "It will be sunny and cool, so let's go!" Mom decided to pack sandwiches, fruit, and a big bottle of juice. Ling, the older sister, said she should bring sunscreen and a hat to protect her skin. Her little brother, Kevin, was too excited to sit still. He kept asking, "When will we leave?"

Before leaving, everyone had to check a list. Dad reminded them, "We must bring a blanket to sit on, and we have to carry enough water for the whole afternoon." Ling grabbed her badminton racket to play with Kevin later.

When they arrived, the riverside park was full of families enjoying the fresh air. They spread the blanket under a shady tree and ate their lunch together. After eating, Kevin ran to the water's edge to look for small fish, while Ling and her parents took a slow walk along the path to enjoy the scenery.

In the afternoon, dark clouds suddenly appeared. "It looks like it will rain soon," Dad said. They quickly packed their things and hurried back to the car. Just as they closed the car doors, big raindrops started to fall. Everyone laughed with relief.

On the way home, Kevin said, "This was the best picnic ever! Next time, we should invite Grandma too." Mom smiled and agreed. Even though the trip ended early, the family had a wonderful day together, full of laughter, fresh air, and happy memories.

## 文法重點

1. **未來式 will（表示預測或臨時決定）**：will + 原形動詞，用來預測未來的事、或說話當下做的決定。例如：*It will be sunny and cool.*；*It looks like it will rain soon.*
2. **情態動詞 should / must / have to（建議與義務）**：should 表溫和建議，must / have to 表較強的義務或必要，其後皆接原形動詞。例如：*She should bring sunscreen.*；*We must bring a blanket.*；*We have to carry enough water.*
3. **不定詞表目的（to + 原形動詞）**：用來說明做某動作的「目的」，意思是「為了…」。例如：*Kevin ran to the water's edge to look for small fish.*；*Ling grabbed her racket to play with Kevin.*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. B — 文中提到「Dad checked the weather report」，Dad 出發前先查看了天氣預報。
2. A — 文中提到 Ling 說要帶防曬乳「to protect her skin」，目的是保護皮膚。
3. B — 文中描述午餐後 Kevin 到水邊找小魚，Ling 和父母則沿著小徑散步欣賞風景。
4. B — 文中提到烏雲突然出現，看起來快要下雨，所以他們趕緊收拾東西回到車上。
5. B — 文中 Kevin 說「Next time, we should invite Grandma too.」，建議下次邀請奶奶一起參加。

**文法**
1. B — will 為助動詞，後面必須接原形動詞，不可加 s 或 to，故正確為 will rain。
2. B — 根據當下看到的徵兆對未來做預測，須用 will + 原形動詞，表示「快要下雨了」。
3. C — must 是情態動詞，後面須直接接原形動詞，不可加 s、to 或 -ing，故正確為 must bring。
4. A — should 用來提出溫和的建議，語氣比 must（必須）更委婉，符合此句語意。
5. C — 不定詞 to + 原形動詞可用來表示動作的目的（為了…），故正確為 to look for fish。

**單字**
1. A — picnic 意指「野餐」。
2. B — sunscreen 意指「防曬乳」。
3. C — riverside 意指「河邊」。
4. B — suddenly 意指「突然地」。
5. B — relief 意指「鬆一口氣；如釋重負」。
