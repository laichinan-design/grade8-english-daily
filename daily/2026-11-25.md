# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-25

**標題：Planning a Surprise Birthday Party**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Today is Lily's birthday, but she doesn't know that her friends are planning a surprise party for her after school. Mia is in charge of decorations. She bought colorful balloons and a big banner that says "Happy Birthday, Lily!" Jack is responsible for the cake. He ordered a chocolate cake with strawberries on top because Lily loves fruit.

"We must keep this a secret," Mia reminded everyone. "If Lily finds out, the surprise will be ruined." Ben offered to invite their classmates. He sent a message to everyone in the class, but he was careful not to let Lily see it.

After school, all the students waited quietly in the classroom with the lights off. When Lily opened the door, everyone shouted, "Surprise!" She was so shocked that she almost dropped her backpack. "I can't believe you did this for me," she said, smiling brightly. They sang the birthday song together and then ate the delicious cake.

Lily received several gifts, including a notebook, a scarf, and a photo album full of memories from their class trips. "You should open this one first," Jack said, handing her a small box. Inside was a bracelet that the whole class had chosen together. Lily was so touched that she almost cried. "This is the best birthday I have ever had," she said. Everyone agreed that the party was a huge success, and they promised to remember this special day forever.

## 文法重點

1. **情態助動詞 must / should (Modal Verbs)**：must 表示「必須」的強烈義務，如 *We must keep this a secret.*；should 表示較溫和的「應該」建議，如 *You should open this one first.*
2. **so + 形容詞 + that 句型**：表示「如此...以致於...」，用來強調程度與結果的關係，如 *She was so shocked that she almost dropped her backpack.*
3. **because 引導原因子句**：because 用來說明原因，後面接一個完整的句子，如 *He ordered a cake because Lily loves fruit.*（他訂蛋糕是因為莉莉喜歡水果）。

## 答案 Key（含解析）

1. **B** — 文中開頭提到 "Today is Lily's birthday"，因為是莉莉的生日，所以朋友們計劃派對。
2. **C** — 文中提到 "Jack is responsible for the cake."，傑克負責蛋糕。
3. **B** — 文中寫 "She was so shocked that she almost dropped her backpack."，她非常震驚。
4. **C** — 文中提到 "Inside was a bracelet that the whole class had chosen together."，小盒子裡是手鍊。
5. **B** — 文中寫 "Lily was so touched that she almost cried."，她感到非常感動。
6. **B** — must 表示「必須」的強烈義務，故選 B。
7. **B** — should 表示語氣較溫和的「建議」，故選 B。
8. **A** — 「so + 形容詞 + that」表示「如此...以致於」，故選 A。
9. **B** — because 引導原因子句，說明訂蛋糕的原因，故選 B。
10. **B** — including 作為介詞表示「包括」，後面接名詞，故選 B。
11. **B** — secret 意思是「秘密」，故選 B。
12. **B** — shocked 是「震驚的」的意思，故選 B。
13. **C** — ruin 意思是「破壞」,符合句意「雨破壞了我們的野餐計畫」，故選 C。
14. **A** — memory 意思是「回憶；記憶」，故選 A。
15. **B** — touched 意思是「感動的」,符合句意「我被他親切的話深深感動」，故選 B。
