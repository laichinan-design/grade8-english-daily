# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-29

**主題：A Trip to the Post Office（去郵局寄包裹）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

On Wednesday afternoon, Ben's mother asked him to go to the post office because she wanted to send a birthday gift to his grandmother in another city. Ben was happy to help because he had never mailed a package by himself before.

When he arrived, the post office was crowded. Ben took a number and waited patiently in line. While he was waiting, he practiced what he wanted to say to the clerk.

"Next, please!" the clerk called. Ben walked up to the counter with the box.

"Hi, I want to send this package to Taichung," Ben said politely. "Could you tell me how much it will cost?"

The clerk weighed the box and checked the address carefully. "It's a little heavy," she said, "so it will cost one hundred and twenty dollars if you want it to arrive by tomorrow."

"That's fine," Ben answered. "My grandmother's birthday is this weekend, so I need it to arrive quickly."

The clerk smiled and printed a receipt. "You can track your package online with this number," she explained, pointing at the paper.

Ben thanked her and paid for the shipping. Before he left, he double-checked the receipt to make sure everything was correct.

On his way home, Ben felt proud because he had solved a problem by himself. He couldn't wait to tell his grandmother that a special surprise was on its way. When he got home, his mother was surprised that he had finished everything so quickly and carefully.

"You did a great job," she said. "Next time, I can trust you to handle it alone."

## 文法重點

1. 不定詞作受詞 (want / need / hope + to V)：want、need、hope 等動詞後面要接「to + 原形動詞」，表示「想要、需要、希望做某事」。例句：I want to send this package to Taichung. / I need it to arrive quickly.
2. 連接詞 because 表原因：because 用來連接「原因子句」，說明為什麼會發生某件事，可放在句中或句首。例句：Ben was happy to help because he had never mailed a package by himself before. / Ben felt proud because he had solved a problem by himself.
3. 情態動詞 can 表能力／允許：can + 原形動詞，表示「能夠」做某事的能力，或表示「可以」做某事的允許。例句：You can track your package online with this number. / I can trust you to handle it alone.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文章開頭提到媽媽要他去郵局，是因為要寄生日禮物給住在外地的奶奶，故答案為 A。
2. 文中寫道 "Ben took a number and waited patiently in line."，表示他很有耐心地等待，故答案為 B。
3. 文中提到店員先秤重並確認地址，之後才告訴他價錢，故答案為 B。
4. Ben 說 "My grandmother's birthday is this weekend, so I need it to arrive quickly."，故答案為 B。
5. 文中提到 "Ben felt proud because he had solved a problem by himself."，故答案為 B。

**文法題**
1. 空格後面說明「幫忙」的原因，應用 because 連接原因子句，故答案為 A。
2. 主詞是 I，want 後面要接 to + 原形動詞，且為現在式對話，故答案為 B。
3. 主詞是 I，need 為一般動詞現在式，後面接 to + 原形動詞，故答案為 A。
4. can 是情態動詞，後面直接加原形動詞，且情態動詞本身不加 s，故答案為 A。
5. 空格後面說明「感到驕傲」的原因，應用 because 連接原因子句，故答案為 A。

**單字題**
1. crowded 意思是「擁擠的」，形容一個地方充滿了人，故答案為 B。
2. patiently 意思是「有耐心地」，指冷靜地等待而不抱怨，故答案為 B。
3. receipt 意思是「收據」，是證明付款的一張紙，故答案為 A。
4. weigh 意思是「秤重」，指測量一件物品有多重，故答案為 B。
5. trust 意思是「信任」，指相信某人誠實可靠，故答案為 B。
