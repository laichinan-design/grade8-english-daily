# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-05

**標題：Finding the Way to the New Bookstore**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Amy just moved to this city last month, and she still gets lost easily. Yesterday, she wanted to find a new bookstore that her classmate told her about, but she had no idea where it was.

"Excuse me," Amy said to a kind stranger near the bus stop. "Could you tell me how to get to Sunshine Bookstore?"

"Sure," the woman said. "Walk straight down this street. When you see the convenience store on the corner, turn left. The bookstore is next to a coffee shop, across from the park."

Amy thanked her and started walking. She passed a bakery and a small flower shop, but she didn't see any bookstore yet. Feeling nervous, she decided to ask again.

"Excuse me, is the bookstore near here?" she asked a boy riding a bike.

"You're almost there," he said with a smile. "Just go straight for two more blocks, and you'll see the sign."

Finally, Amy found Sunshine Bookstore. She felt relieved when she walked through the door. The shelves were full of comic books, novels, and magazines. She picked up a comic book and sat down near the window.

"If you get lost again, just ask someone," the shop owner said kindly. "People here are always happy to help."

Amy smiled. She realized that getting lost wasn't so bad, as long as she wasn't afraid to ask for directions.

## 文法重點

1. **祈使句 (Imperative Sentences)**：給指示或建議時省略主詞，動詞用原形開頭，如 *Walk straight down this street.* / *Turn left at the corner.*
2. **地點介系詞 (Prepositions of Place)**：如 *across from*（在...對面）、*next to*（在...旁邊），用來描述建築物或地點的相對位置。
3. **as long as 句型**：表示「只要...」，用來連接條件與結果，如 *getting lost wasn't so bad, as long as she wasn't afraid to ask*。

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — 文中提到 Amy 想找同學介紹的一家新書店（Sunshine Bookstore）。
2. **A** — 文中女子說書店「next to a coffee shop, across from the park」，在咖啡店旁、公園對面。
3. **B** — Amy 走了一段路仍沒看到書店，所以才又向騎腳踏車的男孩問路。
4. **C** — 文中寫道「She felt relieved when she walked through the door.」，表示她終於放心了。
5. **C** — 文末她意識到「getting lost wasn't so bad, as long as she wasn't afraid to ask for directions」，只要敢問路就不是壞事。

### 文法
1. **A (Walk)** — 祈使句給指示時省略主詞，直接用動詞原形開頭。
2. **C (Turn)** — 祈使句用動詞原形開頭表示指示。
3. **B (across from)** — 表示「在...對面」要用 across from。
4. **B (as long as)** — as long as 表示「只要...」，連接條件與結果。
5. **A (ask)** — 祈使句在 if 子句後仍用動詞原形表示建議/指示。

### 單字
1. **B** — stranger 指不認識的人，即「陌生人」。
2. **A** — get lost 表示「迷路」。
3. **B** — relieved 表示原本擔心的事解決後感到「放心的」。
4. **A** — convenience store 是「便利商店」。
5. **A** — block 指「街區」，用來描述距離。
