# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-11

**標題：** A Rainy Saturday at the Lins' House
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

The Lin family had planned a picnic in the park this Saturday, but when they woke up, it was raining heavily. "If it keeps raining, we will have to change our plan," said Mrs. Lin, looking out the window sadly.

Tim, the younger son, didn't want to stay home all day doing nothing. "Can we bake cookies instead?" he asked. His mother smiled and said, "That sounds like a great idea. Let's do it together."

Mrs. Lin let Tim choose the cookie shapes, and she made him wash his hands carefully before touching the dough. His sister, Amy, offered to help too. She enjoys baking more than cooking, so she felt excited about the plan.

While the cookies were in the oven, the family played a board game at the kitchen table. Mr. Lin, who usually works on Saturdays, had the day off, so he joined them happily. "I don't mind losing this game," he laughed, "as long as we're having fun together."

By the afternoon, the rain had stopped, but nobody wanted to go outside anymore. The kitchen smelled sweet, and everyone felt comfortable just staying in. "If the weather is nice next weekend, we can go on the picnic then," Mrs. Lin said. Tim nodded happily, already looking forward to trying new cookie shapes.

That evening, the family agreed that a rainy day didn't have to be a bad day — it just needed a good plan B.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **第一類條件句 (If + 現在簡單式, ... will/can + 動詞原形)**：表示對未來真實可能發生情況的條件與結果，if 子句用現在簡單式，主要子句用 will 或 can。
   例：*If it keeps raining, we will have to change our plan.* / *If the weather is nice next weekend, we can go on the picnic then.*
2. **使役動詞 let / make + 受詞 + 動詞原形**：let 表示「讓、允許」，make 表示「使、強迫」，後面直接接動詞原形，不加 to。
   例：*Mrs. Lin let Tim choose the cookie shapes.* / *She made him wash his hands carefully.*
3. **enjoy / mind / feel like + 動名詞 (V-ing)**：某些動詞後面需直接接動名詞，而不能接 to + 動詞原形。
   例：*She enjoys baking more than cooking.* / *I don't mind losing this game.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 when they woke up, it was raining heavily，所以他們必須改變原本的野餐計畫。
2. **B** — Tim 問 "Can we bake cookies instead?"，提議改做餅乾。
3. **B** — 文中提到 she made him wash his hands carefully before touching the dough。
4. **B** — 文中提到 Mr. Lin, who usually works on Saturdays, had the day off，所以能跟家人一起。
5. **B** — 文末提到 a rainy day didn't have to be a bad day — it just needed a good plan B。
6. **A** — 第一類條件句的 if 子句要用現在簡單式，故用 keeps。
7. **A** — if 子句用現在簡單式 is，主要子句才用 will/can 表示未來的結果。
8. **B** — let + 受詞 + 動詞原形（不加 to），故用 choose。
9. **C** — make + 受詞 + 動詞原形，表示「使、強迫某人做某事」。
10. **B** — 主詞 She 為第三人稱單數，動詞需加 -s；enjoy 後面要接動名詞 baking，故用 enjoys。
11. **B** — heavily 意為「猛烈地、大量地」，指雨下得又大又急。
12. **B** — offer 意為「主動提議」，指自己主動表示願意幫忙。
13. **B** — dough 意為「麵團」，是用麵粉、水等材料混合而成，用來烘焙的軟質材料。
14. **B** — have the day off 意為「休假一天」，指那天不需要工作。
15. **B** — look forward to 意為「期待」，指對某事感到興奮並希望它快點發生。
