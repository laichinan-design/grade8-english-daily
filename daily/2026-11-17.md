# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-17

**程度**：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)
**標題**：A Class Trip to the Science Museum

## 短文

Last Friday, Mia's class visited the city science museum. Everyone was excited because they had been waiting for this trip for weeks. At the entrance, a friendly guide who worked at the museum welcomed them with a big smile. "Follow me," she said. "There is so much to see that we should start right away."

The first exhibit was a huge dinosaur skeleton. "These bones were discovered in the desert over fifty years ago," the guide explained. Mia stared at the giant skull and felt amazed. Next, they walked into a dark room which was filled with stars and planets. A boy named Tom, who loves astronomy, asked many curious questions about the moon.

In another hall, old scientific instruments were displayed behind glass. "This machine was invented in the nineteenth century," the guide said, pointing at a strange metal device. The exhibit was so interesting that nobody wanted to leave. Mia took notes carefully because her teacher had asked the class to write a report afterward.

Near the end of the trip, the students visited a small theater where a short film about ancient civilizations was shown. The crowd grew quiet as the lights dimmed. Mia whispered to her friend, "This is the best field trip ever!"

Before leaving, the guide gave each student a map of the museum and said, "Come back anytime — there is always something new to discover." On the bus ride home, Mia already knew what her report would be about: the dinosaur that was found so many years ago but still amazes people today.

## 文法重點

1. **關係代名詞（who / which / that）**：用來引導形容詞子句，修飾前面的人或事物。例：A guide **who worked at the museum** welcomed them. / They walked into a dark room **which was filled with stars**.
2. **被動語態（be + 過去分詞）**：強調動作的承受者，而不是動作的執行者，常用於說明事物的來源或發生過程。例：These bones **were discovered** in the desert. / This machine **was invented** in the nineteenth century.
3. **so + 形容詞 + that + 子句**：表示「如此...以致於...」，說明程度造成的結果。例：The exhibit was **so interesting that** nobody wanted to leave.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到「they had been waiting for this trip for weeks」，表示大家已經期待這次校外教學好幾週了。
2. 導覽員說「These bones were discovered in the desert over fifty years ago」，說明這些骨頭是在沙漠中被發現的。
3. 文中提到「A boy named Tom, who loves astronomy, asked many curious questions」，因為湯姆對天文學很感興趣。
4. 文中提到「her teacher had asked the class to write a report afterward」，老師要求全班寫報告。
5. 文末提到「Mia already knew what her report would be about: the dinosaur that was found so many years ago」，米亞打算寫關於那隻恐龍的報告。

**文法**
1. 導覽員是人，修飾人要用關係代名詞 who，故選 A。
2. 骨頭是「被發現」，要用被動語態 were discovered，故選 C。
3. room（房間）是物，修飾事物要用 which，故選 A。
4. 「so + 形容詞 + that」表示「如此...以致於...」，故選 so。
5. 機器是「被發明」的，要用被動語態 was invented，故選 B。

**單字**
1. ancient 意思是「古代的」。
2. amazed 表示「感到驚訝的」，符合看到巨大恐龍骨骸時的反應。
3. crowd 指「群眾」。
4. astronomy 指「天文學」，研究星星與行星的學科。
5. instrument 指「器具、儀器」，此處指科學儀器。
