# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-24

**主題：A Surprise Birthday Party for Grandma（給奶奶的驚喜生日派對）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday, Amy and her brother Jason decided to plan a surprise birthday party for their grandma. She has never had a surprise party before, so they wanted to make this one special.

In the morning, Amy and Jason went to the store to buy balloons and candles. They also invited their cousins and grandma's old friends to celebrate together. "Let's decorate the living room while Grandma is out shopping," Amy said. They worked quickly and quietly so that grandma wouldn't notice anything.

By four o'clock, everyone had gathered at the house. When grandma finally opened the door, all the guests shouted, "Surprise!" Grandma was so surprised that she couldn't say a word for a few seconds. Then she smiled and looked delighted.

"This is more wonderful than any birthday I've ever had," she said happily. Everyone sang a birthday song, and grandma blew out the candles on her cake. She hugged Amy and Jason tightly and thanked them again and again.

"We wanted to create a special memory for you," Jason explained. Grandma felt grateful for such a thoughtful family. The party lasted until evening, and everyone enjoyed the delicious food and cheerful music.

Before the guests left, grandma said, "This year's party was much better than last year's. Thank you for making me so happy." Amy and Jason smiled, knowing that they had made grandma's day unforgettable.

## 文法重點

1. 現在完成式 (Present Perfect: has/have + p.p.)：表示到目前為止的經驗或動作對現在造成的影響，常與 never, ever, already 連用。例如：She has never had a surprise party before.；This is more wonderful than any birthday I've ever had.
2. so...that 結構：表示「如此...以致於...」，句型為 so + 形容詞/副詞 + that + 子句；若接名詞則用 such + (a/an) + 形容詞 + 名詞 + that。例如：Grandma was so surprised that she couldn't say a word for a few seconds.
3. 比較級 (Comparative: more...than / -er than)：用來比較兩者的差異程度，多音節形容詞用 more...than，單音節或部分雙音節形容詞用 -er than，good 的比較級為不規則變化 better。例如：This is more wonderful than any birthday I've ever had.；This year's party was much better than last year's.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Amy and her brother Jason decided to plan a surprise birthday party for their grandma，答案是 A。
2. 文中提到 Amy and Jason went to the store to buy balloons and candles，答案是 B。
3. 文中提到 They worked quickly and quietly so that grandma wouldn't notice anything，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 Grandma was so surprised that she couldn't say a word for a few seconds，答案是 B。
5. 文中提到 This year's party was much better than last year's，答案是 C。

**文法題**
1. 現在完成式的否定副詞 never 要放在 have/has 與過去分詞之間，正確用法為 has never had，故選 B。
2. 主詞 I 搭配現在完成式須用 have + 過去分詞，即 have ever had，故選 A。
3. so + 形容詞 + that + 子句 才是正確句型；such 則需接名詞，故選 B。
4. good 的比較級為不規則變化 better，句型為 better than，故選 B。
5. wonderful 是多音節形容詞，比較級須用 more + 形容詞 + than，故選 A。

**單字題**
1. decorate 意思是「裝飾」，符合布置房間的情境，故選 A。
2. delighted 意為「非常高興的」，符合看到朋友時開心的表情，故選 B。
3. grateful 意思是「感激的」，故選 A。
4. blow out 意思是「吹熄」，過去式為 blew out，故選 A。
5. memory 意思是「回憶」，故選 A。
