# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-01

**主題：A Birthday Gift for Mom（給媽媽的生日禮物）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Amy was going to buy a birthday gift for her mother at City Mall on Saturday. She asked her older brother, Jack, to go with her because she always trusted his opinions.

"What should we get for Mom?" Amy asked as they walked into the mall.

"She usually likes practical things," Jack said. "Maybe a warm scarf or a nice bag?"

"That's a good idea, but I think a cookbook might be more special," Amy replied. "Mom always enjoys trying new recipes on weekends."

They walked past several shops before they found a small bookstore. Amy picked up a colorful cookbook about Italian food. "This book is too heavy to carry around all day," Jack laughed. "Let's buy it first and keep shopping."

At the next shop, they saw a soft blue scarf. "It's too expensive to buy," Amy said, looking at the price tag. They decided to look for something cheaper.

Finally, they found a small flower shop near the exit. Amy chose a bunch of sunflowers because her mom always said sunflowers made her happy. "These flowers are so beautiful that Mom will love them," Jack said with a smile.

When they got home, Amy wrapped the cookbook and the flowers together. She was going to surprise her mom after dinner. That evening, when Mom opened the gift, she was so touched that she hugged both of her children tightly.

"You always know what makes me happy," Mom said. Amy and Jack smiled at each other, proud of their special surprise.

## 文法重點

1. be going to（未來式，表計畫）：be going to + 原形動詞，用來表示事先計畫好、即將發生的未來動作，be 動詞需依主詞與時態做變化（was/were going to 表示過去「原本打算」）。例句：Amy was going to buy a birthday gift for her mother. / She was going to surprise her mom after dinner.
2. too...to...（太...而不能...）：too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞，表示程度過高以致於無法做某事。例句：This book is too heavy to carry around all day. / It's too expensive to buy right now.
3. so...that...（如此...以致於...）：so + 形容詞 + that + 子句，表示程度之深而導致後面的結果。例句：These flowers are so beautiful that Mom will love them. / She was so touched that she hugged both of her children tightly.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 "Amy was going to buy a birthday gift for her mother at City Mall on Saturday."，故答案為 B。
2. 文中提到 Jack 說 "Maybe a warm scarf or a nice bag?"，故答案為 C。
3. 文中提到 "It's too expensive to buy," Amy said，故答案為 B。
4. 文中提到 Amy wrapped the cookbook and the flowers (sunflowers) together，故答案為 B。
5. 文中提到 "she was so touched that she hugged both of her children tightly."，故答案為 C。

**文法題**
1. be going to 表示事先計畫好的未來動作，主詞 Amy（第三人稱單數，過去時態）搭配 was going to，故答案為 A。
2. too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞，表示「太...而不能...」，故答案為 B。
3. so + 形容詞 + that 子句，表示「如此...以致於...」，故答案為 B。
4. too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞，表示「太...而不能...」，故答案為 B。
5. be going to 表示計畫中的未來動作，主詞 She（單數，過去時態）搭配 was going to，故答案為 A。

**單字題**
1. cookbook 意思是「食譜書」，裡面包含許多做菜的食譜，故答案為 B。
2. practical 意思是「實用的」，指有用且合理的，故答案為 B。
3. bunch 意思是「一束/一把」，指捆在一起的一群花，故答案為 B。
4. touched 意思是「感動的」，指情感上深受感動，故答案為 B。
5. hug 意思是「擁抱」，指用手臂環抱某人表達親暱，故答案為 C。
