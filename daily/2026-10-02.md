# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-02

**主題：A Night at the Night Market（夜市之夜）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Friday, Ben and his sister Lucy went to the night market near their house. The market was more crowded than usual because of the summer festival.

"Look at that line!" Lucy said. "The fried chicken stall is the most popular one here."

"Let's try the stinky tofu first," Ben said. "It smells strong, but it tastes amazing."

They walked past rows of stalls selling grilled squid, bubble tea, and sweet potato balls. The smell of spicy sauce filled the air.

At one stall, an old vendor was selling handmade bags. "These bags are cheaper than the ones in the department store," the vendor said with a smile.

Lucy wanted to buy a small bag, but she didn't bring enough cash. "Can you lower the price a little?" she asked. The vendor laughed and gave her a small discount.

Ben was too hungry to wait in the long line, so they chose a quieter stall instead. He ordered a bowl of noodles, and Lucy got a cup of mango shaved ice.

"This is the best shaved ice I've ever had," Lucy said happily.

Before going home, they stopped by a game stall. Ben tried to win a prize by throwing rings, but it was harder than it looked. In the end, they just laughed and decided the fun was more important than the prize.

It was a noisy, crowded, and wonderful evening. Both of them agreed that the night market was definitely worth the trip.

## 文法重點

1. 比較級與最高級 (Comparative and Superlative Adjectives)：形容詞比較用法，多音節形容詞用「more + 形容詞 + than」表較級，如 more crowded than；單音節形容詞加 -er，如 cheaper than、harder than；最高級則表示「最...」，單音節加 -est 或前面加 the most，如 the most popular、the best。例句：The market was more crowded than usual. / This is the best shaved ice I've ever had.
2. too...to...（太...而不能...）：too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞，表示程度過高以致於無法做某事。例句：Ben was too hungry to wait in the long line.

## 答案 Key

**閱讀理解**：1-B　2-B　3-B　4-C　5-C
**文法題**：1-C　2-C　3-B　4-A　5-B
**單字題**：1-A　2-B　3-C　4-B　5-B

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Lucy 想買小包包，但「didn't bring enough cash」（沒帶足夠現金），所以才請小販降價，故答案為 B。
2. 文中寫道「Ben was too hungry to wait in the long line, so they chose a quieter stall instead.」，因為 Ben 太餓了等不了，故答案為 B。
3. Lucy 說「This is the best shaved ice I've ever had.」，表示這是她吃過最好吃的剉冰，故答案為 B。
4. 文中提到「it was harder than it looked」，表示遊戲比看起來的更難，故答案為 C。
5. 文末寫道「the night market was definitely worth the trip」，表示他們認為這趟夜市行很值得，故答案為 C。

**文法題**
1. crowded 是多音節形容詞，比較級須用「more + 形容詞 + than」，故答案為 C。
2. 表達「我吃過最好的...」要用最高級，前面須加 the，故答案為 C。
3. cheap 是單音節形容詞，比較級加 -er，故答案為 B。
4. 「too + 形容詞 + to + 動詞」表示「太...而不能...」，故答案為 A。
5. hard 是單音節形容詞，比較級加 -er，故答案為 B。

**單字題**
1. stall 指市場或夜市裡的「攤位」，故答案為 A。
2. bargain 作動詞時表示「討價還價」，故答案為 B。
3. spicy 表示「辛辣的」，味道嗆辣，故答案為 C。
4. 因為音樂很大聲，所以街道是「吵鬧的」，故答案為 B。
5. vendor 指在市場或路邊販賣商品的「小販」，故答案為 B。
