## 📖 短文：A Birthday Gift for Mom

Next Monday my mom will turn forty, so my sister and I decided to buy her a special gift. We only have five hundred dollars, so we need to find something nice within our budget.

First, we went to a department store that sells scarves, bags, and shoes. My sister, who loves shopping, picked out a pink scarf. "If we buy this scarf, Mom will look great when she wears it to work," she said. I checked the price tag and saw that it was on sale — twenty percent off!

Then we walked past a shop that sells kitchen tools. There was a colorful mug that Mom might like, but it wasn't very useful because she already has too many mugs at home. We decided to skip it.

Finally, we found a small bookstore. The clerk who works there recommended a novel that Mom has wanted to read for months. "If she doesn't like the story, you can exchange it within seven days," the clerk explained kindly. We felt relieved and bought both the scarf and the book.

When we got home, we wrapped the gifts carefully and hid them under my bed. My sister said, "I hope Mom will be surprised, not disappointed, when she opens them!" I told her not to worry because Mom always appreciates gifts that come from the heart.

On the big day, Mom opened the presents slowly. She smiled and said, "These are exactly what I needed! If you keep choosing gifts like this, I'll never need to buy anything for myself again!" We laughed and hugged her together.

## 📐 文法重點（本篇 2 個文法概念）

1. **第一條件句（First Conditional: If + 現在式, ... will/can + 原形動詞）**：表示「有可能發生的未來情況」。
   例：*If we buy this scarf, Mom will look great*、*If she doesn't like the story, you can exchange it*、*If you keep choosing gifts like this, I'll never need to buy anything*。
2. **關係代名詞（Relative Pronouns: who / that）**：
   - 先行詞是「人」→ **who**：*My sister, who loves shopping*、*The clerk who works there*
   - 先行詞是「事物」→ **that**：*a department store that sells scarves*、*a shop that sells kitchen tools*、*a novel that Mom has wanted to read*

## ✅ 答案 Key（作答後再看！）

**1. (B)** — 文中 "We only have five hundred dollars, so we need to find something nice within our budget."，可知預算有限。
**2. (B)** — 姊姊說 "Mom will look great when she wears it to work."
**3. (C)** — 文中 "it wasn't very useful because she already has too many mugs at home."
**4. (B)** — 店員說 "If she doesn't like the story, you can exchange it within seven days."
**5. (D)** — Mom 微笑並說禮物正是她需要的，兩人開心擁抱，屬於 (D) 開心滿足。
**6. (C)** — 第一條件句：If 子句用現在式（buy），主要子句用 will + 原形動詞（will look）。
**7. (B)** — 先行詞 "the clerk" 是人，關係代名詞用 who。
**8. (C)** — 先行詞 "a shop" 是事物，關係代名詞用 that（也可用 which）。
**9. (B)** — on sale 指「特價中、打折」，並非賣光或停賣。
**10. (A)** — 第一條件句主要子句可用 can 或 will + 原形動詞，(B)(C)(D) 文法皆錯誤。
