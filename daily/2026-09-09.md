# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-09

**主題：A Class Trip to the Science Museum（校外教學：科學博物館之旅）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Friday, Mr. Lin's class went on a trip to the City Science Museum. It was the students' first time visiting such a large museum, and everyone was excited before the bus even arrived.

When they entered the main hall, they saw a huge robot that could move its arms and answer simple questions. "I have never seen a robot like this before!" shouted Ben, who was standing at the front of the line.

The class then moved to the space exhibit, which showed models of planets and a real piece of moon rock. Their guide, Ms. Chen, explained how astronauts collect samples in space. She spoke so clearly that even the shyest students asked questions.

After lunch, the students visited the water exhibit to learn how rivers form. Lisa, who loves science, took notes carefully so that she could share them with her sick friend later. "I've always wanted to know how mountains and valleys are created," she said happily.

Before leaving, the students stopped by the gift shop to buy postcards and small souvenirs. Many of them had already decided to come back with their families on the weekend.

On the bus ride home, Ben said, "This is the best field trip I have ever had!" Everyone agreed that the museum trip was both fun and educational, and they couldn't wait to tell their parents about everything they had learned.

## 文法重點

1. **現在完成式（have/has + 過去分詞）**：表示經驗或延續至今的狀態，常與 never、already、ever、always 連用。例如：*I have never seen a robot like this before!*；*This is the best field trip I have ever had!*；*I've always wanted to know how mountains and valleys are created.*
2. **關係代名詞 who / which**：who 指「人」、which 指「物」，用來補充說明先行詞。例如：*Ben, who was standing at the front of the line, shouted with excitement.*；*The space exhibit, which showed models of planets, was very popular.*
3. **so + 形容詞/副詞 + that / so that**：so...that 表示「如此…以致於」，so that 表示「為了；以便」。例如：*She spoke so clearly that even the shyest students asked questions.*；*Lisa took notes carefully so that she could share them with her sick friend later.*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. A — 文中提到「Mr. Lin's class went on a trip to the City Science Museum」。
2. B — 文中提到「they saw a huge robot that could move its arms」。
3. B — 文中提到「their guide...explained how astronauts collect samples in space」。
4. B — 文中提到「Lisa...took notes carefully so that she could share them with her sick friend later」。
5. C — 文中提到「This is the best field trip I have ever had!」。

**文法**
1. A — 現在完成式（have + 過去分詞）表示經驗，主詞 I 搭配 have，seen 為 see 的過去分詞。
2. A — 現在完成式表示「到目前為止」的經驗，主詞 I 搭配助動詞 have。
3. A — 關係代名詞 who 用來指「人」，在此修飾先行詞 Ben。
4. B — 關係代名詞 which 用來指「物」，在此修飾先行詞 the space exhibit。
5. A — so + 形容詞/副詞 + that 表示「如此…以致於」，故填 so / that。

**單字**
1. A — astronaut 意指「太空人」。
2. B — souvenir 意指「紀念品」。
3. C — shy 意指「害羞的」。
4. A — valley 意指「山谷」。
5. B — educational 意指「有教育意義的」。
