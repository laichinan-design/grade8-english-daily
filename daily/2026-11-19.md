## 短文：Learning to Skateboard at the Park

Last Saturday, Jason went to the skate park with his cousin, Eric, who is a skateboard expert. Jason had never tried skateboarding before, so he felt a little nervous. "Don't worry," Eric said. "Everyone falls at the beginning. You must wear a helmet and knee pads before you start." Jason put on his helmet, wrist guards, and knee pads carefully.

At first, Jason could not balance on the board at all. He fell down three times and got a small bruise on his arm. Eric was very patient. He said, "You should bend your knees and look forward, not down at your feet." Jason tried again, and this time he moved a little more smoothly than before.

After an hour of practice, Jason could roll across the park without falling. He was more confident than he was in the morning. Eric smiled and said, "You're getting better every minute! If you keep practicing, you'll be able to do an easy trick next month."

On the way home, Jason told his mom, "Skateboarding is harder than I thought, but it's also more fun than I expected! I've never felt so proud of myself." His mom laughed and said, "That's because you didn't give up. Practice makes perfect!"

That night, Jason searched online for tips for beginners and made a plan to practice every weekend. He hoped that one day he could skateboard as well as his cousin Eric.

## 文法重點

1. **比較級 (Comparative adjectives)**：單音節形容詞加 -er，多音節形容詞用 more + 形容詞，後接 than。
   例：*Skateboarding is harder than I thought.* / *He was more confident than he was in the morning.*
2. **should / must 表建議與義務**：must 表示「必須」（規定、強制），should 表示「應該」（建議，語氣較委婉）。
   例：*You must wear a helmet.*（規定） / *You should bend your knees.*（建議）
3. **現在完成時表經驗 (Present perfect for experience)**：have/has + 過去分詞，強調從過去到現在累積的經驗或感受。
   例：*I've never felt so proud of myself.*

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Jason 的表哥 Eric 教他滑板（with his cousin, Eric, who is a skateboard expert）。
2. Eric 要求 Jason 穿戴安全帽、護腕和護膝（helmet, wrist guards, and knee pads）。
3. 文中提到他一開始跌倒了三次（fell down three times）。
4. 媽媽說是因為他沒有放棄，並說「熟能生巧」(Practice makes perfect)。
5. 文中提到他當晚搜尋線上初學者教學，並計畫每週末練習。

**文法**
1. confident 是多音節形容詞，比較級須用 more + 形容詞 + than，故選 more。
2. must 表示「必須」，用於強調規定或義務，符合句意「這是規則」。
3. should 用來表達建議，語氣比 must 委婉。
4. 現在完成時結構為 have/has + 過去分詞；feel 的過去分詞是 felt，故選 B。
5. hard 是單音節形容詞，比較級為 harder than，而非 more hard than，故選 A。

**單字**
1. bruise 指跌倒或撞到後皮膚上出現的瘀傷。
2. patient 的意思是「有耐心的」。
3. trick 指花招、特技動作，符合句意「表哥能做出驚人的特技」。
4. beginner（初學者）的反義詞是 expert（專家、高手）。
5. encourage 的意思是「鼓勵」。
