# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-17

**主題：A Weekend Camping Trip（週末露營之旅）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last weekend, Ben and his older sister Amy went camping with their uncle, Mr. Chen, who is an experienced hiker. It was Ben's first time sleeping in a tent, and he felt nervous but excited.

When they arrived at the campsite, the tent was too heavy for Ben to carry alone, so Amy helped him. Mr. Chen, who has visited this campsite many times, showed them how to set up the tent quickly. "This place is quiet enough for us to hear the river," he said with a smile.

After dinner, they collected wood and made a campfire. Sitting around the fire, Mr. Chen told stories about animals that he has seen in the mountains, including a fox that once sneaked into his tent. Ben was so surprised that he almost dropped his cup.

Before going to sleep, Amy pointed out the stars, which looked brighter than in the city. "I have never seen so many stars before," she said. Ben agreed and added that the trip was more exciting than he had expected.

The next morning, they packed up their things carefully and cleaned the campsite before leaving. Ben told his uncle that he wanted to go camping again next summer.

## 文法重點

1. too...to / adj. + enough (for sb.) to V：too + 形容詞 + to V 表示「太…以致於不能」；形容詞 + enough (for sb.) + to V 表示「足夠…可以」。例如：the tent was too heavy for Ben to carry alone.；This place is quiet enough for us to hear the river.
2. 關係代名詞 who / which 補充說明子句：who 用來補充說明「人」，which 用來補充說明「事物」，前後常加逗號插入額外資訊，去掉該子句句意仍完整。例如：Mr. Chen, who has visited this campsite many times, showed them how to set up the tent.；The stars, which looked brighter than in the city, amazed Amy.
3. 現在完成式表經驗 (have/has + 過去分詞)：表示從過去某時到現在為止的經驗，常與 never、before、many times 等字連用。例如：Mr. Chen has visited this campsite many times.；I have never seen so many stars before.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Ben and his older sister Amy went camping with their uncle, Mr. Chen，答案是 C。
2. 文中提到 the tent was too heavy for Ben to carry alone，答案是 A。
3. 文中提到 Mr. Chen told stories about animals that he has seen in the mountains，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 Amy pointed out the stars, which looked brighter than in the city，答案是 C。
5. 文中提到 they packed up their things carefully and cleaned the campsite before leaving，答案是 B。

**文法題**
1. 「too + 形容詞 + for sb. + to V」表示「太…以致於某人不能…」，故選 too。
2. 「形容詞 + enough (for sb.) + to V」表示「足夠…可以」，enough 須放在形容詞之後，故選 enough。
3. 先行詞 Mr. Chen 是「人」，且該關係代名詞在子句中作主詞，須用 who。
4. 先行詞 the stars 是「事物」，且該關係代名詞在子句中作主詞，須用 which。
5. 與 before 連用表示「從過去到現在的經驗」，須用現在完成式 have never seen。

**單字題**
1. experienced 意指「有經驗的」，符合句意「因多次做某事而具備技巧與知識」。
2. campsite 意指「露營地」，是人們搭帳篷過夜的地方。
3. sneak 意指「偷偷地行動、溜」，符合句意「悄悄移動不被發現」。
4. point out 意指「指出」，符合句意「清楚地提及或指出某事」。
5. pack up 意指「打包收拾」，符合句意「把東西收拾好準備離開」。
