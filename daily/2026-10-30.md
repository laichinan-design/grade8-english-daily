# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-30

**標題：** A Group Project for the Science Fair
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

On Thursday afternoon, Mia and her two classmates, Leo and Amy, stayed after school to work on their science project. They wanted to build a model that shows how volcanoes erupt.

"This project is so exciting that I can't wait to show it to the class," Mia said, mixing baking soda in a plastic cup.

"I agree, but I'm too tired to finish painting the mountain today," Leo said, putting down his paintbrush.

"Don't worry. Amy is the student who is best at art in our class. She can help us tomorrow," Mia suggested.

Amy smiled. "I'd love to help. The teacher who gave us this project said creative ideas get extra points."

Suddenly, their experiment bubbled over the table. "Oops! It's too messy to clean up alone," Leo laughed, grabbing some paper towels.

"Let's work together. Many hands make light work," Mia said cheerfully, and they all began cleaning.

After an hour, the model looked amazing. "This volcano is so realistic that it might scare some kids," Amy joked.

"I'm so proud of our teamwork," Mia said. "A good partner is someone who shares both the fun and the hard work."

The next day, their volcano model won first prize at the science fair. The judge, who had seen hundreds of projects, said theirs was the most creative one of the year.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **so + 形容詞 + that 子句**：表示程度高到產生某種結果。
   例：*This project is so exciting that I can't wait to show it to the class.* / *This volcano is so realistic that it might scare some kids.*
2. **too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞**：太...以致於不能做某事。
   例：*I'm too tired to finish painting the mountain today.* / *It's too messy to clean up alone.*
3. **關係代名詞 who（修飾人）**：引導形容詞子句，代替前面提到的人。
   例：*Amy is the student who is best at art in our class.* / *The judge, who had seen hundreds of projects, said theirs was the most creative one.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到他們要做一個展示火山爆發的模型（a model that shows how volcanoes erupt）。
2. **B** — Leo 說「I'm too tired to finish painting the mountain today」，表示他太累了。
3. **B** — Mia 說 Amy 是班上最擅長藝術的學生，可以幫忙。
4. **B** — 他們的實驗在桌上冒出並溢出（bubbled over the table）。
5. **B** — 評審說他們的作品是當年最有創意的一件（the most creative one of the year）。
6. **A** — "so + 形容詞 + that 子句" 表示程度高到產生某結果，故選 A。
7. **B** — "too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞" 表示太...以致於不能，故選 B。
8. **B** — 關係代名詞 who 用來修飾人，故選 B。
9. **A** — "too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞" 結構正確，故選 A。
10. **B** — who 修飾人（the judge），故選 B。
11. **A** — erupt 意為「爆發」。
12. **B** — creative 意為「有創意的」。
13. **B** — messy 意為「凌亂的」。
14. **A** — teamwork 意為「團隊合作」。
15. **B** — realistic 意為「逼真的」。
