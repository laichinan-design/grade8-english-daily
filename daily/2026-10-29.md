# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-29

**標題：** A New Exchange Student at School
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

On Monday morning, a new student named Yuki joined Class 8A. She came from Japan and looked a little nervous.

"Hi, I'm Yuki. I used to study in Tokyo, but my family moved to Taiwan last month," she said shyly.

"Welcome to our class! Taiwan's weather is probably hotter than Tokyo's, right?" Tom asked with a smile.

"Yes, it is much hotter here. I used to wear a heavy coat in winter, but I don't need one now," Yuki laughed.

During lunch, Tom introduced Yuki to his friends. "This is the most delicious noodle shop near our school," he said, pointing at the cafeteria.

"It smells amazing! I used to eat sushi almost every day, but I want to try new food here," Yuki said happily.

In P.E. class, Yuki ran faster than most of the boys. "Wow, you're the fastest runner in our class!" Tom said in surprise.

"I used to be on the track team in Japan," Yuki explained. "Running makes me feel relaxed."

After school, Tom and his friends invited Yuki to join their basketball club. "It's more fun than studying alone," one classmate said.

Yuki smiled and nodded. "I think this will be the best year of my life. Thank you for being so kind to me."

## 📚 文法重點

1. **used to + 原形動詞**：表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已不再如此。
   例：*I used to study in Tokyo.* / *I used to wear a heavy coat in winter, but I don't need one now.*
2. **比較級 (Comparative)**：`形容詞 + -er + than`，比較兩者。
   例：*Taiwan's weather is hotter than Tokyo's.* / *It's more fun than studying alone.*
3. **最高級 (Superlative)**：`the + 形容詞 + -est` / `the most + 形容詞`，表示三者以上最高程度。
   例：*This is the most delicious noodle shop near our school.* / *You're the fastest runner in our class!*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 Yuki 來自日本（She came from Japan）。
2. **B** — Yuki 說「it is much hotter here」，表示台灣比東京熱很多。
3. **B** — Tom 向 Yuki 介紹學校附近「最好吃的麵店」(the most delicious noodle shop)。
4. **B** — Yuki 在體育課中跑得比大部分男生快，讓 Tom 感到驚訝。
5. **B** — 放學後 Tom 和朋友邀請 Yuki 一起加入籃球社。
6. **A** — "used to + 原形動詞" 表示過去的習慣，現在已不再如此，故選 A。
7. **C** — 單音節形容詞比較級加 -er，hot → hotter，故選 C。
8. **C** — 多音節形容詞最高級為 the most + 形容詞，故選 C。
9. **A** — "used to + 原形動詞" 描述過去習慣，與現在對比時現在用一般現在簡單時，故選 A。
10. **C** — fast 為單音節形容詞，最高級加 -est，故選 C。
11. **A** — nervous 意為「緊張的」。
12. **B** — introduce 意為「介紹」。
13. **B** — amazing 意為「令人驚奇的；了不起的」。
14. **B** — relaxed 意為「放鬆的」。
15. **B** — kind 意為「親切的；善良的」。
