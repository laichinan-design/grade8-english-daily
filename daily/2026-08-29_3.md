## A Night at the Night Market

Last Saturday, my cousin Amy came to visit us from Taichung, so my brother and I decided to take her to the night market near our house. She had never been to a night market in our city before, and she was very excited.

When we arrived, the street was already more crowded than we expected. Bright lights, sizzling oil, and the smell of grilled squid filled the air. "Everything smells delicious!" Amy said. We walked past stalls selling bubble tea, fried chicken, and sticky rice. My brother, who loves spicy food, bought a bowl of spicy noodles and ate it in two minutes.

Amy wanted to try the claw machine, but she had never played one before. After she lost three times, my brother gave her some tips, and she finally won a small toy. "If you turn the joystick slowly, you will have a better chance," he explained.

We also visited a stall where an old man sold handmade souvenirs. Amy bought a bracelet for her mother because it was cheaper than the one at the mall. By nine o'clock, we had walked around the whole market and tried at least six different snacks.

On the way home, Amy said this was the most exciting night she had had in months. We agreed to go back again next month, but next time we will arrive earlier so we can avoid the biggest crowds.

## 文法重點 (Grammar Focus)

1. **過去完成式 (Past Perfect)**：had + p.p.，表示「在過去某個時間點之前」已發生或未發生的動作。例：`She had never been to a night market before.` / `this was the most exciting night she had had`。
2. **比較級與最高級 (Comparative & Superlative)**：形容詞 + er / more + 形容詞（比較級），the + 形容詞 + est / the most + 形容詞（最高級）。例：`more crowded than`、`cheaper than`、`the most exciting`。
3. **第一類條件句 (First Conditional)**：If + 現在簡單式, ... will + 原形動詞，表示「若…則會…」的未來可能結果。例：`If you turn the joystick slowly, you will have a better chance.`

## 答案 Key（僅供批改，勿貼上對話視窗）

1. B — Amy 來自 Taichung（台中），文中第一句提到。
2. B — 文中提到 "After she lost three times"，她輸了三次才贏。
3. B — 文中提到手鍊 "cheaper than the one at the mall"，因為比較便宜。
4. C — 文中提到 "tried at least six different snacks"。
5. C — 文中最後提到 "next time we will arrive earlier so we can avoid the biggest crowds"。
6. B — 過去完成式：had + p.p.，表示在造訪夜市之前從未去過。
7. A — 比較級：more + 形容詞（crowded 較長，用 more）+ than。
8. A — 第一類條件句：If + 現在簡單式, ... will + 原形動詞。
9. B — stall 意指「攤位」，其餘為 crowd(人群)/bracelet(手鍊)/souvenir(紀念品)。
10. B — 最高級：the most + 形容詞，表示「最令人興奮的」。
