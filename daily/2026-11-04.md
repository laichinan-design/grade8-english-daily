# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-04

**標題：** A Weekend Hike in the Mountains
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Last Saturday, Lily and her father decided to go hiking in the mountains near their town. They woke up early and packed a backpack with water, snacks, and a map. "Have you ever hiked up Sun Moon Trail before?" her father asked. "No, this is my first time," Lily said excitedly.

The trail was longer and steeper than they expected. After an hour, Lily felt exhausted and wanted to stop. "Let's take a short rest," her father suggested. "You should drink some water and eat a snack." They sat under a big tree and looked at the beautiful view below.

As they climbed higher, the air became cooler and the path became narrower. Lily's father used a compass to make sure they were going the right direction. "This path is more difficult than the one we took last year," he said. "But the view from the top will be worth it."

Finally, after three hours, they reached the summit. The view was amazing — they could see the whole valley and a shining lake in the distance. "This is the best hike I have ever taken!" Lily shouted happily. Her father smiled and took a photo to remember the adventure.

On their way down, Lily asked, "Can we come back next month?" Her father laughed and said, "Of course! Next time, we can bring your little brother, too." They walked home, tired but happy, already planning their next adventure together.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **比較級 (adj + -er / more + adj) + than**：規則變化加 -er（longer, steeper），多音節形容詞用 more + 形容詞（more difficult）。
   例：*The trail was longer and steeper than they expected.* / *This path is more difficult than the one we took last year.*
2. **現在完成時 Have/Has + ever + 過去分詞**：表示「曾經做過某事」，常與 ever 連用。
   例：*Have you ever hiked up Sun Moon Trail before?* / *This is the best hike I have ever taken!*
3. **should 表建議**：should 為情態動詞，後面直接加動詞原形，表示「應該」。
   例：*You should drink some water and eat a snack.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 Lily 和爸爸一起去健行（Lily and her father）。
2. **C** — 文中寫到健行一小時後「Lily felt exhausted」(感到疲憊不堪)。
3. **C** — 文中提到爸爸使用指南針(compass)確認方向。
4. **C** — 文中提到「after three hours, they reached the summit」(三小時後抵達山頂)。
5. **B** — 下山途中 Lily 問爸爸「Can we come back next month?」(我們下個月可以再來嗎)。
6. **B** — 規則比較級形容詞，直接加 -er，long → longer。
7. **A** — 多音節形容詞 difficult 的比較級要用 more + 形容詞。
8. **C** — 現在完成時的疑問句用 Have/Has + 主詞 + 過去分詞，表示「你曾經...嗎」。
9. **A** — 主詞 I 搭配現在完成時用 have + 過去分詞 (have taken)。
10. **A** — should 為情態動詞，後面直接加動詞原形，不加 to。
11. **B** — steep 意為「陡峭的、難以攀爬的」。
12. **C** — exhausted 意為「非常疲憊的」。
13. **C** — summit 意為「山頂、頂峰」。
14. **B** — worth 意為「值得的」。
15. **B** — compass 是「指南針」，用來辨別方向。
