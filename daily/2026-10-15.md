# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-15

**標題：A Lost Wallet at the Night Market**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Tom and his sister Amy go to the night market every Thursday evening. The night market, which is near their house, sells many kinds of delicious food and cheap clothes.

Tonight, Amy wants to buy a bubble tea, but when she opens her bag, she can't find her wallet. "Oh no! My wallet is missing!" she says, feeling very nervous.

Tom tells her, "Don't panic. If we ask the stall owners, they will help us look for it." They walk back to the stalls they visited and ask each owner carefully.

At the last stall, the owner who sells fried chicken smiles and says, "A wallet was found by a kind customer a few minutes ago. It is kept at the information booth now." Amy feels relieved and runs to the booth.

The wallet, which contains her ID card and some cash, is returned to her safely. "Thank you so much!" Amy says happily.

Tom laughs and says, "If you keep your wallet in your pocket next time, you won't lose it again!" They walk through the crowded night market together, enjoying the bright lights and delicious smells.

## 文法重點

1. **關係代詞 who / which 引導形容詞子句**：who 修飾人，which 修飾事物，用來補充說明前面的名詞，如 *The night market, which is near their house, sells many kinds of food.* / *The owner who sells fried chicken smiles and says...*
2. **被動語態 (be + 過去分詞)**：強調動作的承受者而非執行者時使用，如 *A wallet was found by a kind customer.* / *The wallet is kept at the information booth now.*
3. **第一條件句 (if + 現在簡單式, 主詞 + will + 原形動詞)**：表示現實中可能發生的情況與其結果，如 *If we ask the stall owners, they will help us look for it.* / *If you keep your wallet in your pocket, you won't lose it again.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — Amy 打開包包時發現錢包不見了，所以沒辦法買珍珠奶茶。
2. **C** — Tom 說「If we ask the stall owners, they will help us look for it.」，建議去問攤位老闆。
3. **C** — 文章提到「A wallet was found by a kind customer a few minutes ago.」，是一位好心的顧客找到的。
4. **B** — 文章提到錢包「is kept at the information booth now」。
5. **B** — Tom 最後說「If you keep your wallet in your pocket next time, you won't lose it again!」。

### 文法
1. **B (which)** — which 引導形容詞子句修飾事物（night market），並補充說明其位置。
2. **C (who)** — who 引導形容詞子句修飾人（the owner）。
3. **C (was found)** — 被動語態 be + 過去分詞，強調錢包被找到這個動作的承受者。
4. **C (will help)** — 第一條件句：if + 現在簡單式，主要子句用 will + 原形動詞。
5. **A (won't lose)** — 第一條件句的否定形式，won't + 原形動詞表示「將不會」。

### 單字
1. **B (nervous)** — nervous 意為「緊張的」，符合找不到錢包時的心情。
2. **A** — panic 意為「恐慌、驚慌」。
3. **B (crowded)** — crowded 意為「擁擠的」，用來形容夜市人多的樣子。
4. **B** — return 意為「歸還」。
5. **B (information booth)** — information booth 意為「服務台」，可在此尋求協助。
