# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-10

**主題：A Day at the Amusement Park（遊樂園一日遊）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

On Saturday morning, Amy and her brother Jack went to Sunshine Amusement Park with their parents. It was the biggest park they had ever visited, and there were so many rides to choose from.

"Let's ride the roller coaster first! It's the most exciting ride here," said Jack, pointing at a huge red track.

Before getting on the roller coaster, they had to wait in a long line. While waiting, Amy read the safety rules on a sign: "Please fasten your seatbelt. Don't stand up during the ride."

After finishing the roller coaster, Jack's face turned pale. "That was faster than I expected!" he said, laughing nervously. Amy suggested trying something calmer, so they walked over to the Ferris wheel instead.

From the top of the Ferris wheel, they could see the whole park. "This is the tallest ride I've ever been on," Amy said happily, taking photos of the view.

Before leaving, the family stopped at a food stall to buy popcorn and cold drinks. They also visited the gift shop, where Jack bought a small toy dinosaur as a souvenir.

On the way home, Jack said, "This was the most fun day of my summer!" Amy agreed and said she couldn't wait to come back with her friends next time.

## 文法重點

1. **最高級（the + 形容詞-est / the most + 形容詞）**：表示「最…的」，用來比較三者以上。例如：*It's the most exciting ride here.*；*This is the tallest ride I've ever been on.*；*This was the most fun day of my summer!*
2. **祈使句（Imperative Sentences）**：動詞原形開頭表示指示、規定或請求，否定用 Don't/Do not + 原形動詞。例如：*Please fasten your seatbelt.*；*Don't stand up during the ride.*
3. **比較級（形容詞-er / more + 形容詞 + than）**：表示兩者之間「比較…」。例如：*That was faster than I expected!*；*Amy suggested trying something calmer.*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. A — 文中提到「Amy and her brother Jack went to Sunshine Amusement Park」。
2. B — 文中提到 Jack 說「Let's ride the roller coaster first!」。
3. B — 文中提到告示牌上寫著「Please fasten your seatbelt.」。
4. B — 文中提到「Jack's face turned pale」。
5. C — 文中提到「Jack bought a small toy dinosaur as a souvenir」。

**文法**
1. A — 表示「最刺激的」需用最高級 the most exciting（exciting 為多音節形容詞，最高級用 the most + 原級）。
2. C — the + 形容詞-est 表示最高級，tall 為單音節形容詞，最高級加 -est 為 tallest。
3. A — 祈使句以動詞原形開頭表示指示或請求，故填 Fasten。
4. B — 祈使句的否定形式為 Don't/Do not + 原形動詞，故填 Don't。
5. B — 比較級用來比較兩者，fast 為單音節形容詞，比較級加 -er 為 faster，後接 than。

**單字**
1. B — Ferris wheel 意指「摩天輪」。
2. A — exciting 意指「刺激的；令人興奮的」。
3. B — seatbelt 意指「安全帶」。
4. A — stall 意指「攤位」。
5. C — pale 意指「蒼白的」。
