# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-14

**標題：A New Haircut Before the Talent Show**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Lily is going to perform in the school talent show next week, so she decides to get a new haircut. She has never visited this hair salon before, but her friend Mia told her that the stylist here is very skilled.

The stylist, Mr. Chen, asks Lily what style she wants. "I used to have long hair, but now I want something shorter and easier to take care of," Lily says.

Mr. Chen smiles and picks up his scissors. "Don't worry. I have cut hair for more than ten years. I will make sure your hair looks great for the show."

After thirty minutes, Mr. Chen finishes. Lily looks at her reflection in the mirror and is so surprised that she can hardly believe it is her own face. The new style is short, neat, and modern.

"Wow, I love it!" Lily says happily. "It's so different from my old hairstyle that none of my classmates will recognize me!"

Mia, who has just arrived to pick her up, laughs and says, "You look amazing! You should wear this style to the talent show for sure."

Lily pays for the haircut, thanks Mr. Chen, and leaves the salon feeling confident. She can't wait to perform on stage and show everyone her new look at the talent show.

## 文法重點

1. **現在完成時 (have/has + 過去分詞)**：表示從過去持續到現在的經驗或動作，常與 never、for、since 連用，如 *She has never visited this salon before.* / *He has cut hair for more than ten years.*
2. **used to + 原形動詞**：表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已不再如此，如 *She used to have long hair, but now it's short.*
3. **so + 形容詞/副詞 + that...**：表示「如此...以致於...」的結果，如 *She is so surprised that she can hardly believe it.* / *It's so different that none of her classmates will recognize her.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — 文章開頭提到 Lily 下週要在才藝表演中表演，所以決定剪新髮型。
2. **C** — 文章提到「her friend Mia told her that the stylist here is very skilled」。
3. **B** — Lily 說「I used to have long hair」，表示她以前頭髮是長的。
4. **C** — Mr. Chen 說「I have cut hair for more than ten years.」
5. **A** — 文章最後提到 Lily「leaves the salon feeling confident」。

### 文法
1. **C (has cut)** — 表示從過去到現在持續的經驗，用現在完成時 has cut。
2. **B (used to)** — used to + 原形動詞表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已不再如此。
3. **C (so)** — so + 形容詞 + that 表示「如此...以致於...」。
4. **B** — 現在完成時為 has/have + 過去分詞，never 放在 has/have 和過去分詞之間，第三人稱單數用 has。
5. **B (easier)** — 兩者比較用比較級，easy 結尾 y 改 ier。

### 單字
1. **B (stylist)** — stylist 指「髮型設計師」，是負責詢問並剪髮的人。
2. **B** — neat 意為「整潔的」，用來形容乾淨俐落的髮型。
3. **A (skilled)** — skilled 意為「熟練的、有技術的」，形容經驗豐富的師傅。
4. **B** — recognize 意為「認出」，例如朋友認不出改變造型後的自己。
5. **A (pick up)** — pick up 意為「接某人」，Mia 來接 Lily 回家。
