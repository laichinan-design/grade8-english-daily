# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-07

**標題：Calling to Book Movie Tickets**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Tom wanted to watch a new movie with his sister, Lucy, this weekend, so he decided to call the cinema to check the show times.

"Hello, this is Sunshine Cinema. How can I help you?" the clerk asked.

"Hi, I'd like to know what time the new superhero movie starts on Saturday," Tom said.

"We have three showtimes: 2:00 p.m., 5:00 p.m., and 8:00 p.m. Which one would you prefer?" the clerk replied.

"The 5:00 p.m. show sounds perfect. Could you please book two tickets for me?" Tom asked politely.

"Sure. Do you have to pay online, or would you like to pay at the counter?" the clerk said.

"I don't have to pay online, right? I'll pay when I arrive," Tom said.

"That's fine. You just have to arrive fifteen minutes early to collect your tickets," the clerk explained.

"Great! My sister and I are going to meet our friends there, too," Tom said happily.

"Sounds like fun! Don't forget your student ID for the discount," the clerk reminded him.

"Thanks a lot! I won't forget," Tom said before hanging up the phone.

On Saturday afternoon, Tom and Lucy arrived at the cinema early. They bought some popcorn and drinks before the movie started. The movie was more exciting than they had expected, and everyone cheered at the end. After the movie, they talked about their favorite scenes all the way home.

## 文法重點

1. **have to / don't have to**：表示情態動詞「必須」與「不必」，have to 表示義務，don't have to 表示沒有必要（但仍可以做），如 *You have to arrive early.* / *I don't have to pay online.*
2. **Could you please + 原形動詞**：禮貌請求的句型，語氣比 Can you... 更客氣，如 *Could you please book two tickets for me?*
3. **be going to + 原形動詞**：表示事先安排好的未來計畫，如 *My sister and I are going to meet our friends there.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — Tom 打電話去電影院是為了詢問場次並訂票。
2. **B** — Tom 說「The 5:00 p.m. show sounds perfect」。
3. **C** — Tom 說他不必線上付款，到現場時再付錢即可。
4. **A** — 店員提醒 Tom 別忘了帶學生證以享折扣。
5. **B** — 文中提到「They bought some popcorn and drinks before the movie started」。

### 文法
1. **A (have to)** — 表示「必須」，主詞是 You，故用原形 have to。
2. **B (don't have to)** — 表示「不必」，主詞是 I，用 don't have to。
3. **A (Could)** — Could you please... 是禮貌請求的常見句型。
4. **B (are going to)** — 主詞是 My sister and I（複數），be 動詞用 are。
5. **B (was)** — 全文以過去式敘述，故用 was。

### 單字
1. **A** — cinema 指「電影院」。
2. **A** — discount 指「折扣」。
3. **B** — remind 意思是「提醒」。
4. **A** — hang up 指「掛斷電話」。
5. **A** — favorite 後面接名詞複數 scenes，表示「場景」。
