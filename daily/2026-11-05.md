# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-05

**標題：** A Surprise Birthday Party for Grandma
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Next Saturday is Grandma's seventieth birthday, and Tom's family is planning something special. "We are going to throw a surprise party for Grandma," Mom said. "But we must keep it a secret!" Tom felt excited. He had never planned a party before.

On Friday night, everyone got busy. Tom and his sister decorated the living room with colorful balloons and a big banner that said "Happy Birthday!" Dad ordered a chocolate cake, Grandma's favorite flavor. "We have to finish everything before she comes home," Dad reminded them.

Mom called Grandma and said, "Can you come to our house at six o'clock tomorrow? We will have a quiet dinner together." Grandma did not know about the real plan at all.

On Saturday, all the relatives arrived early and hid behind the sofa. At exactly six o'clock, Grandma opened the door. Everyone jumped up and shouted, "Surprise! Happy Birthday!" Grandma was so surprised that she almost dropped her bag. "I can't believe you did all this for me," she said, smiling with tears in her eyes.

They sang songs, ate the delicious cake, and shared old family stories. Grandma said, "This is the best birthday I have ever had. Thank you all so much." Tom felt proud. He realized that planning a party is hard work, but seeing Grandma's happy face made it worth every minute.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **be going to / will 表示未來計畫或預測**：be going to 強調事先安排好的計畫；will 可表示當下決定或承諾。
   例：*We are going to throw a surprise party for Grandma.* / *We will have a quiet dinner together.*
2. **must 與 have to 表示「必須」**：must 常表示說話者主觀認為的必要；have to 表示客觀上的需要或規定，第三人稱單數變成 has to。
   例：*We must keep it a secret.* / *We have to finish everything before she comes home.* / *She has to leave early.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到「Next Saturday is Grandma's seventieth birthday」，是慶祝奶奶的七十歲生日。
2. **B** — Mom 說「But we must keep it a secret!」，表示必須保密。
3. **C** — 文中提到 Dad ordered a chocolate cake，那是 Grandma's favorite flavor。
4. **A** — Mom 在電話中說「Can you come to our house at six o'clock tomorrow? We will have a quiet dinner together.」
5. **C** — 文中提到 Grandma was so surprised that she almost dropped her bag，顯示她非常驚訝。
6. **A** — be going to 用來表示事先安排好的未來計畫，故選 are going to。
7. **B** — must 表示「必須」，強調說話者認為非常重要的義務。
8. **A** — have to 表示客觀上必須做的事，後面直接加動詞原形，故選 have to（must to / will to 為錯誤用法）。
9. **B** — will 可用來表示當下所做的承諾或未來的預測，故選 will。
10. **B** — 主詞 Grandma 為第三人稱單數，have to 要變成 has to，故選 has。
11. **B** — surprise 指「出乎意料、令人驚訝的事」。
12. **B** — decorate 意為「裝飾」，使空間看起來更漂亮。
13. **B** — secret 指「不讓其他人知道的事」，即秘密。
14. **B** — relative 意為「親戚」，指有血緣或婚姻關係的家人。
15. **B** — proud 意為「感到自豪、滿意」，通常因自己做了值得驕傲的事而產生。
