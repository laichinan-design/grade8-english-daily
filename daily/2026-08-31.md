## A Big Clean-Up Day

This Saturday morning, Mrs. Chen told her two children, Tom and Lily, "Our house is very messy. We must clean it together today." Tom groaned, but he knew he had to help.

First, Mom gave everyone a job. "Tom, you should sweep the floor in the living room. Lily, you have to fold the laundry in your room. I will clean the kitchen," she said. "We don't have to finish everything before lunch, but we must try our best."

Lily has already washed all her dirty clothes, but she has never folded so many shirts before. "This is harder than I thought!" she said. Tom has swept the floor twice because he found more dust under the sofa.

By noon, they had cleaned most of the house. Mom looked around and smiled. "You have both done a great job today. We should take a short break now and eat lunch together."

After lunch, Dad came home from the market with fresh vegetables. "You must be tired," he said. "You don't have to do any more chores today. Let's watch a movie tonight instead!"

Tom and Lily were happy to hear this. They have learned that cleaning together is faster and more fun than cleaning alone. "We should clean the house every weekend," Lily said with a smile. Mom laughed and said, "That's a wonderful idea!"

## 文法重點 (Grammar Focus)

1. **情態動詞 must / have to / should / don't have to**：must 表示強烈義務、have to 表示客觀上的需要、should 表示建議、don't have to 表示「不必要」（並非「不可以」）。
2. **現在完成式 (have/has + 過去分詞)**：表示動作對現在造成的影響或經驗，常與 already、never、twice 等副詞連用，如 has already washed、has never folded、has swept twice。
3. **比較級**：單音節形容詞字尾加 -er（faster、harder），用來比較兩件事物的程度。

## 答案 Key 與中文解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 "Our house is very messy. We must clean it together today."，媽媽要求全家一起打掃房子。
2. **C** — 文中提到 "Lily, you have to fold the laundry in your room."
3. **B** — 文中提到 "Tom has swept the floor twice because he found more dust under the sofa."
4. **C** — 文中提到 "Dad came home from the market with fresh vegetables."
5. **B** — 文中提到 "Let's watch a movie tonight instead!"
6. **B** — don't have to 表示「不必要」，符合「不一定要做但做了很好」的語意；must / mustn't 表示強制或禁止，語氣不符。
7. **A** — 主詞 Lily 為第三人稱單數，現在完成式為 has + 過去分詞，故答案為 has washed。
8. **C** — should 後面直接加原形動詞，不可加 to、不可變化、也不可加 -ing。
9. **B** — 兩者比較用比較級，fast 為單音節形容詞，比較級加 -er，故為 faster。
10. **C** — must 表示強烈的義務或必要性，語氣比 should（建議）更強。
11. **B** — groan 意指「呻吟、不情願地發出聲音」，符合小孩被叫去打掃時不情願的反應。
12. **A** — chore 指「家務事、雜務」。
13. **B** — messy 意指「凌亂的、雜亂的」，符合語意。
14. **A** — dust 意指「灰塵」。
15. **B** — break 在此作名詞，意指「休息（時間）」。
