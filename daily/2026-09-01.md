## A Weekend Trip to the Zoo

On Friday evening, Amy called her friend Ben on the phone. "Hi Ben, are you free this weekend? I'm going to visit the zoo with my cousin. Do you want to join us?" she asked.

Ben was excited. "Sure! I love animals, especially the ones that live in the rainforest. What time will we meet?"

"We will meet at the bus stop at nine o'clock," Amy said. "The bus that goes to the zoo leaves every thirty minutes, so we shouldn't be late."

Ben checked the weather forecast on his phone. "It says it might rain in the afternoon. Should we bring umbrellas?"

"That's a good idea," Amy replied. "I'll pack some snacks too, in case we get hungry. My cousin, who works at an animal hospital, knows a lot about wild animals. She can tell us interesting facts."

On Saturday morning, the three friends met at the bus stop. The weather was sunny, but dark clouds were forming in the sky. "I think it's going to rain later," Ben said, looking up. They walked into the zoo and saw elephants, tigers, and monkeys that were playing near a small pond.

Suddenly, it started to rain. They quickly opened their umbrellas and laughed. "I'm glad we brought these!" Amy said. After the rain stopped, they visited the reptile house, which had colorful snakes and lizards.

By the end of the day, everyone agreed that it was one of the best weekends they had ever had. "Let's plan another trip next month," Ben suggested, and they all happily agreed.

## 文法重點 (Grammar Focus)

1. **未來式 will vs. be going to**：be going to 表示事先計畫好的打算，或根據眼前跡象判斷即將發生的事，如 *I'm going to visit the zoo*、*dark clouds...it's going to rain later*；will 表示說話當下的臨時決定、單純的未來預測或承諾，如 *We will meet at the bus stop*、*I'll pack some snacks too*。
2. **關係代名詞 who / which / that**：who 代替「人」作子句主詞，如 *my cousin, who works at an animal hospital*；which 代替「物」作子句主詞，常用於補充說明的子句，如 *the reptile house, which had colorful snakes*；that 可代替人或物，常用於限定子句，如 *the bus that goes to the zoo*、*monkeys that were playing*。
