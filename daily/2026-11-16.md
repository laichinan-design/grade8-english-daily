# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-16

**程度**：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)
**標題**：Ken's First Visit to the Dentist

## 短文

Ken used to be afraid of the dentist. Every time his mother made an appointment, he felt nervous and tried to find an excuse to stay home. "If you skip your check-up, your teeth will get worse," his mother said kindly. "A small cavity is much easier to fix than a big one."

On Monday afternoon, Ken finally went to the clinic with his mother. The receptionist smiled and said, "Please wait here. The dentist will call your name soon." Ken looked around the waiting room. It was quieter than he had expected, and the walls were painted with cheerful cartoons.

When Dr. Lin examined his teeth, she moved slowly and explained every step. "This will only take a few minutes, and it won't hurt," she said. "If you brush twice a day and floss every night, your teeth will stay healthy." Ken nodded and tried to relax.

After the check-up, Dr. Lin smiled. "Good news — no cavities today! Your teeth are healthier than last year." Ken felt proud of himself. On the way home, he told his mother, "I used to hate coming here, but it wasn't so bad after all. Next time, I won't be so nervous."

That night, Ken brushed his teeth carefully before bed. He realized that a little bit of courage could make a difficult task much easier than he had imagined.

## 文法重點

1. **used to + 動詞原形**：表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已經不再如此。例：Ken used to be afraid of the dentist, but now he isn't.
2. **第一類條件句（If + 現在簡單式, ... will + 動詞原形）**：表示「如果...就會...」，說明真實可能發生的未來結果。例：If you brush twice a day, your teeth will stay healthy.
3. **比較級（形容詞 + er / more + 形容詞 + than）**：用來比較兩者之間的差異程度。例：Your teeth are healthier than last year. / A small cavity is much easier to fix than a big one.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 "Ken used to be afraid of the dentist"，他過去很怕看牙醫，所以一聽到要預約就緊張，想找藉口不去。
2. 媽媽說「If you skip your check-up, your teeth will get worse.」意思是如果不去檢查，牙齒狀況會惡化。
3. 文中寫道 "It was quieter than he had expected"，候診室比他原本想的安靜許多。
4. 醫生說「Good news — no cavities today!」表示這次檢查沒有發現蛀牙。
5. 最後一段提到他明白「a little bit of courage could make a difficult task much easier than he had imagined」，一點點勇氣就能讓困難的事變得容易許多。

**文法**
1. 「used to + 動詞原形」表示過去的習慣，動詞要用原形 be，故選 C。
2. 第一類條件句結構為「If + 現在簡單式, 主句 will + 動詞原形」，故選 will stay。
3. 形容詞 healthy 以 y 結尾，比較級要去 y 加 ier，變成 healthier，故選 B。
4. 「used to + 動詞原形」表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已經不再如此，故選 used to。
5. 第一類條件句中 If 子句用現在簡單式(rains)，主句要用 will + 動詞原形，故選 will cancel。

**單字**
1. nervous 意思是「緊張的」，符合句意：他在第一次看牙醫前感到緊張。
2. cavity 指「蛀牙」，是牙齒因細菌侵蝕而形成的洞。
3. examine 意思是「檢查」，符合句意：牙醫會仔細檢查你的牙齒再給建議。
4. courage 意思是「勇氣」。
5. floss 作動詞指「用牙線清潔牙齒」。
