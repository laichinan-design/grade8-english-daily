# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-03

**主題：A Night at the Night Market（夜市之夜）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday evening, Amy and her cousin Ben went to the night market near their house. The market was so crowded that they could hardly walk through it. Bright lights and sizzling sounds came from every stall.

"There are so many stalls here that I don't know where to start," Ben said, looking around hungrily.

"Let's ask the vendor for advice," Amy suggested. She stopped at a stall selling stinky tofu. "Excuse me, could I have one order of stinky tofu, please? And what would you recommend for us?"

The vendor smiled. "The stinky tofu here is very popular. It's crispy outside and soft inside. I would also recommend our spicy noodles. They aren't too greasy, and many customers love the sour sauce on top."

Amy and Ben didn't have much time, so they ordered quickly. They also bought a bag of grilled squid and a cup of sweet bubble tea. The squid was so hot that Ben almost dropped it!

While they were eating, they wandered past dozens of stalls selling clothes, toys, and games. Ben wanted to try a shooting game, but Amy reminded him that they didn't have much money left.

"We spent so much on food that we should save the rest," Amy said with a laugh. "Let's just enjoy watching people play."

By the end of the night, they were both stuffed and tired, but happy. "This was such a delicious trip," Ben said. "Can we come back next weekend?"

"Of course," Amy replied. "There are always new stalls worth trying at the night market."

## 文法重點

1. **much / many / a lot of**：可數名詞複數用 many，不可數名詞用 much，兩者皆可用 a lot of。例如：*there are so many stalls*（stalls 為可數名詞複數，用 many）；*they didn't have much time / much money*（time、money 為不可數名詞，常用於否定句與疑問句，用 much）。
2. **Would like to V / Could I have...?**：用來禮貌地表達「想要」或提出「請求」，比直接說 want 或 can 更客氣。例如：*I would also recommend our spicy noodles.*；*Could I have one order of stinky tofu, please?*（比 Can I have...? 更有禮貌）。
3. **so + 形容詞/副詞 + that 子句**：表示「如此…以致於…」，強調程度造成的結果，that 不可省略。例如：*The market was so crowded that they could hardly walk through it.*（so + 形容詞 crowded + that 引導結果子句）；*The squid was so hot that Ben almost dropped it!*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. A — 文中提到「Amy and her cousin Ben went to the night market near their house」。
2. B — 攤販說「I would also recommend our spicy noodles.」。
3. B — 文中提到「The squid was so hot that Ben almost dropped it!」。
4. C — 文中提到 Amy 提醒他們「they didn't have much money left」。
5. B — 文中提到「they were both stuffed and tired, but happy」。

**文法**
1. B — stalls 為可數名詞複數，故用 many。
2. B — time 為不可數名詞，常用於否定句，故用 much。
3. B — Could I have...? 為禮貌的請求用法。
4. C — so + 形容詞 + that 子句表示「如此…以致於…」。
5. A — so...that 句型中的 that 用來連接結果子句，不可省略。

**單字**
1. A — crowded 意指「擁擠的」。
2. B — recommend 意指「推薦」。
