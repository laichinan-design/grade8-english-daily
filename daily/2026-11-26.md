# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-26

**標題：A Beach Cleanup on Saturday Morning**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday, Ms. Lin organized a beach cleanup for our class, and more than thirty students joined the activity. We met at the school gate at eight in the morning and took a bus to Sunrise Beach.

When we arrived, we noticed a lot of litter near the rocks—plastic bottles, bags, and even old shoes. Our teacher gave everyone a pair of gloves and a big bag. "We should protect the environment," she said. "The ocean is home to many sea animals."

We worked in teams of five. By noon, we had collected more than fifteen bags of trash! The beach looked much cleaner than before. Some students found plastic that will take hundreds of years to break down, so we talked about why we must recycle more and use less plastic in our daily lives.

After the cleanup, we ate lunch at a nearby noodle shop. Everyone was exhausted, but we felt proud of what we had done together. My friend Amy said, "I have never worked so hard in my life, but it was worth it."

On the bus home, our teacher asked us to write down one thing we learned. I wrote: small actions can make a big difference. I hope more people will volunteer for activities like this in the future, because every piece of litter we pick up helps keep our beautiful island clean.

## 文法重點

1. **現在完成時 (have/has + 過去分詞)**：表示從過去持續到現在、或剛完成的動作，如 *We have collected more than fifteen bags of trash.*、*I have never worked so hard in my life.*
2. **形容詞比較級 (-er than / more...than)**：用來比較兩者的差異，如 *The beach looked much cleaner than before.*
3. **情態動詞 should / must 表義務或建議**：should 表示「應該」(建議)，must 表示「必須」(強烈義務)，如 *We should protect the environment.*、*We must recycle more.*

## 答案 Key（含解析）

1. **B** — 文中提到「Ms. Lin organized a beach cleanup for our class」，表示活動是林老師籌辦的。
2. **C** — 文中寫到「we had collected more than fifteen bags of trash」，答案為 C。
3. **B** — 文中提到「we ate lunch at a nearby noodle shop」，答案為 B。
4. **B** — 文中提到塑膠「will take hundreds of years to break down」，所以答案為 B。
5. **B** — 文中結尾寫到「small actions can make a big difference」，答案為 B。
6. **C** — 表示到中午「已經」完成的動作，須用現在完成時 have + 過去分詞，故選 C。
7. **B** — clean 是單音節形容詞，比較級須加 -er，故為 cleaner than，答案為 B。
8. **B** — 現在完成時 have + 過去分詞，work 的過去分詞為 worked，故選 B。
9. **A** — should 表示建議或義務「應該」，句意為「我們應該保護環境」，答案為 A。
10. **B** — must 表示強烈的義務「必須」，語氣比 should 更強烈，答案為 B。
11. **A** — litter 意為「垃圾、廢棄物」，符合句意，答案為 A。
12. **A** — volunteer 作動詞意為「自願做」，作名詞意為「志工」，答案為 A。
13. **B** — gloves 意為「手套」，撿垃圾時需要戴手套保護雙手，答案為 B。
14. **B** — exhausted 意為「疲憊不堪的」，答案為 B。
15. **A** — volunteer 作動詞意為「當志工、自願做」，符合句意，答案為 A。
