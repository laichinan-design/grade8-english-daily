# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-28

**主題：Asking for Directions to the Train Station（問路到火車站）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday, Lily visited her cousin in a city she had never been to before. She wanted to take the train home in the afternoon, but she didn't know how to get to the train station. She decided to ask a stranger for help.

"Excuse me," Lily said politely. "Could you tell me how to get to the train station?"

"Sure," the man answered with a smile. "Have you ever been to this station before?"

"No, this is my first time," Lily replied.

"Okay, then listen carefully," he said. "Walk straight ahead until you reach the traffic light. Turn left and go past the bakery. The station is just across from the park."

"Is it far from here?" Lily asked. "I don't want to miss my train."

"Not at all," he said. "If you walk quickly, it will only take about ten minutes. You should also use the crosswalk when you cross the busy street."

Lily thanked the man and followed his directions carefully. She walked along the road, passed several shops, and finally saw the station in front of her. She checked the schedule on the board and found her train would leave in fifteen minutes.

At the ticket machine, she felt a little nervous because it was her first time buying a ticket by herself. Luckily, a friendly staff member helped her choose the right ticket.

When Lily got on the train, she felt proud of herself. "I have never traveled alone before," she thought, "but today I learned that asking for help is not embarrassing at all." She smiled and looked out the window, excited to tell her parents about her small adventure.

## 文法重點

1. 現在完成式 (Present Perfect: have/has + p.p.)：用於表示「到目前為止的經驗」，常與 ever / never 連用，強調「有沒有做過某事」而非特定時間點。例句：Have you ever been to this station before?；I have never traveled alone before.
2. 祈使句 (Imperative Sentences)：用於給指示、建議或命令，句子開頭直接使用原形動詞，省略主詞 you。例句：Walk straight ahead until you reach the traffic light.；Turn left and go past the bakery.
3. 情態動詞 should（建議）：表示「應該」，用來給予建議或提醒對方該做的事。例句：You should also use the crosswalk when you cross the busy street.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 She wanted to take the train home in the afternoon，故選 A。
2. 文中提到 She decided to ask a stranger for help，故選 B。
3. 文中提到 You should also use the crosswalk when you cross the busy street，故選 C。
4. 文中提到 She felt a little nervous because it was her first time buying a ticket by herself，故選 D。
5. 文中提到 asking for help is not embarrassing at all，故選 B。

**文法題**
1. 詢問「有沒有經驗」須用現在完成式疑問句 Have + 主詞 + 過去分詞，主詞是 you，故答案為 B（Have）。
2. 指路用的祈使句須用原形動詞開頭，省略主詞 you，故答案為 C（Walk）。
3. 祈使句表示指示或命令，動詞須用原形，故答案為 D（Turn）。
4. should 表示「應該」，用來給予建議，故答案為 A（should）。
5. 主詞 I 搭配現在完成式須用 have + p.p.，故答案為 C（have）。

**單字題**
1. politely 意思是「有禮貌地」，符合向陌生人問路時的語氣，故答案為 A。
2. stranger 意思是「陌生人」，故答案為 B。
3. across from 意思是「在...的對面」，符合火車站與公園相對位置的敘述，故答案為 A。
4. nervous 意思是「緊張的」，符合第一次自己買票時的心情，故答案為 B。
5. adventure 意思是「冒險；奇遇」，故答案為 C。
