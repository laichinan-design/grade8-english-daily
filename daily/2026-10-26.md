# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-26

**標題：** Cooking with Grandma on a Rainy Afternoon
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

It was raining heavily on Saturday afternoon, so Mia decided to visit her grandmother and learn how to cook noodles.

"Today I'll teach you my special recipe," Grandma said with a smile. "First, wash the vegetables carefully."

Mia nodded and washed the carrots and green onions in the sink. "What should I do next?" she asked.

"Next, chop the vegetables into small pieces," Grandma explained. "Be careful with the knife."

Mia chopped slowly, trying to follow every step. After that, Grandma boiled water in a big pot and added the noodles.

"How long should we boil them?" Mia asked.

"About five minutes. While we wait, let's stir-fry the vegetables," Grandma said.

They poured oil into the pan and stirred the vegetables quickly. The kitchen soon filled with a delicious smell.

"Don't forget to turn off the stove when the noodles are ready," Grandma reminded her.

Finally, they mixed the noodles with the vegetables and a special sauce. Mia tasted a bite and her eyes lit up with surprise.

"This is the best noodles I've ever had!" she said happily.

"You did a great job," Grandma said. "Next time, you can cook for the whole family."

Mia smiled, feeling proud. She realized that cooking with patience and care could turn a simple rainy afternoon into something special.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **祈使句 (Imperative sentences)**：以動詞原形開頭，用於下指令或給步驟，不加主詞；否定用 `Don't + 原形動詞`。
   例：*Wash the vegetables carefully.* / *Don't forget to turn off the stove.*
2. **步驟連接詞 (Sequence connectors)**：`First`、`Next`、`After that`、`Finally` 用於描述步驟順序，幫助讀者理解先後關係。
   例：*First, wash the vegetables. ... After that, Grandma boiled water... Finally, they mixed the noodles.*
3. **助動詞 should 表建議/詢問做法**：疑問句語序為 `Wh- + should + 主詞 + 原形動詞`。
   例：*What should I do next?* / *How long should we boil them?*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到下大雨時 Mia 決定去奶奶家學做麵（learn how to cook noodles）。
2. **B** — 奶奶先說「wash the vegetables carefully」，Mia 先洗菜，之後才切菜。
3. **B** — 文中提到「Don't forget to turn off the stove when the noodles are ready,」Grandma reminded her.
4. **C** — 文中提到她的眼睛因驚喜發亮（lit up with surprise），並開心地說這是她吃過最好吃的麵。
5. **B** — 文末提到「cooking with patience and care could turn a simple rainy afternoon into something special.」
6. **B** — 祈使句以動詞原形開頭，不加主詞，故選 B。
7. **B** — 否定祈使句用 Don't + 原形動詞，故選 B。
8. **B** — After that 表示「在那之後」，承接前一步驟（洗切蔬菜）之後才煮水下麵，故選 B。
9. **B** — should 為助動詞，疑問句語序為 Wh- + should + 主詞 + 原形動詞，故選 B。
10. **B** — 疑問句語序為 How long + should + 主詞 + 原形動詞，故選 B。
11. **A** — recipe 意為「食譜」，此處指奶奶教 Mia 的特製食譜。
12. **B** — careful 意為「小心的」，提醒使用刀具要小心。
13. **B** — stir-fry 意為「快炒」，符合快速翻炒蔬菜的情境。
14. **B** — remind 意為「提醒」，奶奶提醒 Mia 要記得關火。
15. **A** — proud 意為「驕傲的；自豪的」，符合被稱讚後感到自豪的情境；delicious 用於形容食物，patience 為名詞不可直接當補語，故不選。
