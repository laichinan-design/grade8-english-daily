# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-27

**標題：** A Video Call with Grandpa in Canada
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Last Sunday evening, Tina was sitting in the living room, waiting for her weekly video call with Grandpa, who moved to Canada last year.

"Hi, Tina! Can you hear me clearly?" Grandpa asked, waving at the screen.

"Yes, Grandpa! You look great today," Tina said happily.

"It's much colder here than in Taiwan," Grandpa said, pointing out the window. "The snow is falling outside right now."

"Wow, it's as hot as summer here!" Tina laughed. "What are you doing these days?"

"I'm learning to cook Canadian dishes, and I'm also planting flowers in the garden," Grandpa said proudly.

"That sounds more exciting than staying at home!" Tina replied. "When are you going to visit us?"

"I'm going to fly back next winter," Grandpa said. "I miss your mom's cooking so much."

"We miss you too!" Tina said. "Mom is cooking dinner right now. Do you want to say hello to her?"

"Of course! Please pass the phone to her," Grandpa said with a smile.

Tina ran to the kitchen, holding the phone carefully. "Mom, Grandpa wants to talk to you!"

Her mother wiped her hands quickly and took the phone. The whole family felt warm and connected, even though they were far apart.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **現在進行時 (Present continuous)**：`be + V-ing`，表示當下正在進行的動作。
   例：*The snow is falling outside right now.* / *Mom is cooking dinner right now.*
2. **比較級 (Comparatives)**：形容詞加 `-er + than` 或 `more + 形容詞 + than` 表示「比...更」；`as + 形容詞 + as` 表示「和...一樣」。
   例：*It's much colder here than in Taiwan.* / *That sounds more exciting than staying at home!* / *It's as hot as summer here!*
3. **be going to 表未來計畫**：`be + going to + 原形動詞`，用於談論已決定的未來計畫。
   例：*I'm going to fly back next winter.* / *When are you going to visit us?*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 Tina 在客廳等待她每週與爺爺的視訊電話（weekly video call）。
2. **A** — Grandpa 說「It's much colder here than in Taiwan... The snow is falling outside right now.」
3. **B** — Grandpa 提到他正在學做加拿大菜，也在花園種花。
4. **C** — Grandpa 說「I'm going to fly back next winter.」
5. **B** — 文中提到 Tina 小心地拿著電話跑到廚房找媽媽。
6. **B** — 現在進行時為 be + V-ing，描述當下正在發生的動作，故選 B。
7. **A** — 比較級用「形容詞-er + than」，cold 的比較級是 colder，故選 A。
8. **B** — 「as + 形容詞原級 + as」表示「和...一樣」，故選 B。
9. **B** — be going to 結構為 be + going to + 原形動詞，故選 B。
10. **B** — 疑問句語序為 Wh- + be + 主詞 + going to + 原形動詞，故選 B。
11. **B** — weekly 意為「每週的」，指每週固定進行的事。
12. **C** — proudly 意為「自豪地」，符合爺爺分享種花成果時的語氣。
13. **B** — 此處 miss 意為「想念」，表示爺爺很想念媽媽做的菜。
14. **B** — carefully 意為「小心地」，符合謹慎拿著手機的情境。
15. **B** — connected 意為「連結的；有聯繫的」，指全家人即使分隔兩地仍感到彼此相連。
