# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-18

**主題：A Weekend at the Animal Shelter（動物收容所的週末）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday, Ben went to the city animal shelter to volunteer for the first time. He used to think taking care of dogs was easy, but he quickly learned it was a big responsibility.

When Ben arrived, a worker named Ms. Lin showed him around. Some dogs were sleeping quietly, but others barked loudly at every stranger who walked by. "Don't worry," said Ms. Lin. "If you stay calm and gentle, the dogs will trust you soon."

Ben's first job was to feed the dogs and give each one fresh water. He put a leash on a small, nervous dog named Coco to take her for a walk. Coco used to live on the street as a stray before she was rescued last month. At first, she was too scared to leave her cage, but Ben spoke softly to her so that she would feel safe.

By the afternoon, Ben was exhausted, but he felt proud. "If more people come to adopt these dogs, they can finally have a real home," Ms. Lin said. Ben smiled and promised to come back next weekend.

On the bus home, Ben told his sister about his day. "It was hard work," he said, "but it was completely worthwhile. I used to think shelters were sad places, but now I know they're full of hope."

## 文法重點

1. 假設語氣 First Conditional（If + 現在式動詞, ... will + 原形動詞）：表示條件成立時，很可能發生的未來結果。If 子句用現在式動詞，主要子句用「will + 原形動詞」。例如：If you stay calm and gentle, the dogs will trust you soon.；If more people come to adopt these dogs, they can finally have a real home.；If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
2. used to + 原形動詞：表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已經不同或不再如此，後面一定接原形動詞。例如：Ben used to think taking care of dogs was easy.；Coco used to live on the street as a stray.；I used to think shelters were sad places.
3. 不定詞表目的 (to + 原形動詞) 與 so that + 子句表目的：兩者都可說明做某事的目的。「to V」直接接在動詞後方；「so that + 子句」則引導一個完整的子句來說明目的。例如：Ben went to the shelter to volunteer for the first time.；He put a leash on Coco to take her for a walk.；Ben spoke softly to her so that she would feel safe.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中第一句提到 Ben went to the city animal shelter to volunteer for the first time，答案是 B。
2. 文中提到 Ben's first job was to feed the dogs and give each one fresh water，答案是 C。
3. 文中提到 Coco used to live on the street as a stray before she was rescued，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 By the afternoon, Ben was exhausted, but he felt proud，答案是 C。
5. 文中提到 It was hard work, but it was completely worthwhile，答案是 B。

**文法題**
1. 假設語氣的條件句要用 If 開頭，表示「如果」，後方主要子句用 will + 原形動詞，故選 If。
2. 「used to + 原形動詞」表示過去的習慣或狀態，故選 used to。
3. so that + 完整子句用來表示「目的」，其餘選項後面不能直接接完整子句，故選 so that。
4. 不定詞 to + 原形動詞可表示目的，相當於中文「去做某事」，故選 to。
5. 第一類條件句的 if 子句要用現在式動詞，故選 come。

**單字題**
1. shelter 指提供保護或住所的地方，文中指「動物收容所」。
2. stray 意指「流浪的、無家可歸的」，最接近 lost and homeless。
3. 句意指 Ben 到下午時感到「精疲力盡」，應選 exhausted。
4. adopt 意指「領養」，例如領養一隻寵物。
5. gentle 意指「溫柔的、溫和的」。
