# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-08

**主題：Tom Takes the Wrong Bus（搭錯公車的一天）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

After school on Monday, Tom was waiting at the bus stop with his classmate, Amy. He was checking his phone while waiting for the number 20 bus. Suddenly, a bus stopped in front of them, and its number looked like 20, so Tom jumped on quickly.

"Wait, are you sure this is the right bus?" Amy asked, but Tom was already inside, and the doors closed before she could finish.

While the bus was moving through the city, Tom looked out the window and felt confused. The streets didn't look familiar at all. He checked the number above the driver's seat again — it was 26, not 20!

Tom was too surprised to speak at first. He had been texting his friend and hadn't looked carefully. Now the bus was going in the wrong direction, and it was much slower than his usual bus because it stopped at every corner.

He decided to get off at the next stop and call his mother. "I took the wrong bus by mistake," he explained. "Don't worry, Mom. I'll take the MRT home instead. It's faster than waiting for another bus."

His mother laughed gently. "Next time, check the number before you get on. Being careful is more important than being fast."

Tom finally arrived home, tired but relieved. He learned an important lesson: always look twice before jumping onto a bus. From now on, he promised to be more careful whenever he traveled alone.

## 文法重點

1. **過去進行式（was/were + V-ing）**：表示過去某時間點正在進行的動作，常與 while 連用。例如：*He was checking his phone while waiting for the bus.*；*While the bus was moving through the city, Tom looked out the window.*
2. **too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞**：表示「太…以致於不能…」。例如：*Tom was too surprised to speak at first.*
3. **比較級（-er than / more + 形容詞 + than）**：表示兩者之間的比較。例如：*It was much slower than his usual bus.*；*It's faster than waiting for another bus.*；*Being careful is more important than being fast.*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. B — 文中提到「Tom was waiting at the bus stop with his classmate, Amy」。
2. A — 文中提到「He had been texting his friend and hadn't looked carefully」。
3. B — 文中提到「it was 26, not 20」。
4. C — 文中提到「I'll take the MRT home instead」。
5. B — 文中提到「always look twice before jumping onto a bus」。

**文法**
1. A — 過去進行式（was + V-ing）表示過去某時正在進行的動作，故用 was checking。
2. A — while 子句常搭配過去進行式描述背景動作，故用 was moving。
3. A — too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞表示「太…以致於不能…」，故填 too / to。
4. A — 比較級句型 -er + than 用來比較兩者，故填 than。
5. A — more + 形容詞 + than 為比較級句型，故填 than。

**單字**
1. A — confused 意指「困惑的」。
2. B — direction 意指「方向」。
3. A — explain 意指「解釋」。
4. C — relieved 意指「鬆了一口氣的」。
5. C — lesson 意指「教訓；課」。
