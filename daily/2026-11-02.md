# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-02

**標題：** A Weekend Trip to the Night Market
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

On Saturday evening, Jason's family decided to visit the night market near their town. "I will try the stinky tofu this time," Jason said excitedly, looking at the busy food stalls.

"You have to finish your homework before we leave," Mom reminded him. "We don't have much time, so hurry up."

Jason quickly packed his bag and finished his last math question. "Finally! I don't have to worry about homework anymore," he said with a big smile.

At the market, bright lights and delicious smells filled the air. Tina, Jason's sister, pointed at a stall selling fresh fruit. "I will buy some mango juice. It smells amazing!"

"We must be careful with our money," Dad said. "We don't have to buy everything we see."

They walked past toy stalls, clothing stores, and a game booth where kids threw rings onto bottles. Jason tried three times but didn't win anything. "Don't worry," the shop owner said kindly. "You can try again next week."

After eating noodles, grilled squid, and sweet potato balls, the family sat on a bench to rest. "I'm looking forward to coming back here," Tina said happily. "I also enjoy trying new street food every time."

"Next weekend, we will visit the market on the other side of town," Dad suggested. "It has an even bigger night market with more game booths."

Jason nodded with excitement. "I can't wait! I will save my allowance so I can buy a special gift for Grandma."

On their way home, everyone agreed that the night market trip was the best part of their weekend. They were already looking forward to their next adventure together.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **will 表示未來的預測、決定或承諾**：用於談論說話當下做的決定或對未來的計畫。
   例：*I will try the stinky tofu this time.* / *Next weekend, we will visit the market on the other side of town.*
2. **have to / don't have to 表示必須 / 不必**：have to 表示「必須」做某事，don't have to 表示「不必」做某事。
   例：*You have to finish your homework before we leave.* / *We don't have to buy everything we see.*
3. **look forward to + V-ing 表示期待某事**：to 後面接動詞的 -ing 形式，不可接原形動詞。
   例：*I'm looking forward to coming back here.* / *They were already looking forward to their next adventure together.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — Jason 想和家人一起去逛夜市，所以趕快寫完功課。
2. **B** — Tina 說她要買芒果汁（mango juice），因為聞起來很香。
3. **B** — Jason 玩套圈圈遊戲，試了三次都沒贏，但老闆鼓勵他下次再來。
4. **B** — 爸爸建議下週末去城鎮另一邊更大的夜市。
5. **B** — Jason 說他要存零用錢，買禮物送給奶奶。
6. **B** — "will" 用於表達說話當下做出的決定，故選 B。
7. **C** — "have to + 原形動詞" 表示必須做某事，主詞為 You 時用 have to，故選 C。
8. **A** — "don't have to + 原形動詞" 表示不必做某事，故選 A。
9. **B** — "look forward to" 後面須接動詞 -ing 形式，故選 B。
10. **B** — "will" 用於描述未來的計畫，故選 B。
11. **A** — remind 意為「提醒」。
12. **B** — allowance 意為「零用錢」。
13. **A** — stall 意為「攤位」。
14. **A** — adventure 意為「冒險」。
15. **B** — kindly 意為「親切地」。
