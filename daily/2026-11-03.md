# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-03

**標題：** A New Classmate from Canada
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Today was Lily's first day at a new school. She had just moved from Canada, and everything felt different. "Our classroom here is bigger than my old one," she said to her neighbor, Kevin. "But the weather is much hotter than in Canada!"

Kevin smiled. "You should bring a water bottle every day. It gets hotter in summer, so you must drink enough water."

During lunch, Lily looked nervous. "I don't know anyone here," she whispered.

"Don't worry," Kevin said kindly. "You shouldn't feel shy. Everyone is friendly, and I'm sure you'll make friends quickly."

At recess, Lily joined a basketball game with some classmates. She wasn't as skillful as the other players, but she tried her best and scored twice. "That was amazing!" her classmate Amy shouted. "You're almost as good as our team captain!"

After school, Lily and Kevin walked home together. "Taiwan is noisier than Canada, but I actually like it," Lily said. "The night markets are more exciting than anything near my old house."

"You should visit the one near my house this weekend," Kevin suggested. "It has the best dumplings in town."

Lily smiled brightly. "I'd love that. Thank you for being so kind today."

By the end of the day, Lily felt much happier than in the morning. She realized that making new friends wasn't as hard as she had expected. She couldn't wait to see her new classmates again tomorrow.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **比較級 (adj + -er / more + adj) + than**：表示兩者程度不同的比較。
   例：*Our classroom here is bigger than my old one.* / *The night markets are more exciting than anything near my old house.*
2. **as + 形容詞 + as（同等比較）／not as...as（不如）**：表示兩者程度相同或不相同。
   例：*You're almost as good as our team captain!* / *She wasn't as skillful as the other players.*
3. **should / shouldn't 表建議**：should 表示「應該」，shouldn't 表示「不應該」，用來給建議或忠告。
   例：*You should bring a water bottle every day.* / *You shouldn't feel shy.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **A** — Lily 剛從加拿大(Canada)搬來，到新學校就讀。
2. **B** — Kevin 建議 Lily 每天帶水壺，因為天氣炎熱需要多喝水。
3. **B** — 下課休息時間，Lily 和同學一起打籃球。
4. **B** — Kevin 建議這週末一起去他家附近的夜市，那裡有最好吃的水餃。
5. **B** — 文章最後提到 Lily 一天結束時比早上開心許多（much happier than in the morning）。
6. **B** — 比較級用「形容詞 + -er + than」，big 的比較級為 bigger，故選 B。
7. **B** — 多音節形容詞的比較級用「more + 形容詞 + than」，exciting 前應加 more，故選 B。
8. **A** — 同等比較用「as + 形容詞 + as」，故選 A。
9. **B** — 否定同等比較用「not as + 形容詞 + as」，故選 B。
10. **B** — should/shouldn't 後面接動詞原形，表建議，故選 B。
11. **B** — nervous 意為「緊張的」。
12. **B** — whisper 意為「低聲說」。
13. **A** — friendly 意為「友善的」。
14. **B** — recess 意為「休息時間」。
15. **A** — realize 意為「意識到」。
