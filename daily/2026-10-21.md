# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-21

**標題：Learning to Ride a Bicycle in the Park**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文 (Passage)

Last Saturday morning, Lily's dad took her to the park to practice riding her new bicycle. Lily felt nervous because she wasn't used to riding without training wheels. "Don't worry," her dad said. "You just need to balance and keep your eyes forward."

At first, Lily was afraid of falling off, so she rode very slowly. She wore a helmet to stay safe. Her dad ran beside her and held the seat gently. Suddenly, he let go, but Lily didn't notice—she was already riding by herself!

"Dad, look at me!" Lily shouted happily. She felt more confident than before. It took her about thirty minutes to learn how to ride without help. Her dad smiled and said, "I'm so proud of you! You improved quickly."

After that, Lily practiced every weekend. She became braver and more careful on busy paths. Her little brother, Tim, watched and said, "I want to learn too! Can you teach me, Lily?"

"Of course," Lily said. "It's easier than you think. You just need to practice and never give up." Tim smiled and put on his helmet.

Now Lily rides her bicycle to school every day. Riding a bike is more exciting than walking, and it makes her feel free. Her dad always encourages her to try new things, and Lily is thankful for his patience and support.

## 文法重點

1. **be used to + 動詞-ing/名詞**：表示「習慣於...」，否定則用 wasn't/isn't used to，如 *Lily wasn't used to riding without training wheels.* / *I'm used to getting up early.*
2. **比較級形容詞**：單音節加 -er（braver），多音節用 more + 形容詞（more confident, more careful, more exciting），如 *Riding a bike is more exciting than walking.*
3. **It takes (某人) + 時間 + to V**：表示「花多少時間做某事」，如 *It took her about thirty minutes to learn how to ride.* / *It takes me an hour to do my homework.*

## 閱讀理解 (Reading) — 5題

1. Why did Lily feel nervous at first?
   A) She didn't have a bicycle.  B) She wasn't used to riding without training wheels.  C) Her dad was angry with her.  D) It was raining outside.
   **答案：B**
   解析：文中提到 "Lily felt nervous because she wasn't used to riding without training wheels"，表示她還不習慣沒有輔助輪騎車。

2. What did Lily wear to stay safe while riding?
   A) Gloves  B) A helmet  C) Boots  D) A jacket
   **答案：B**
   解析：文中寫 "She wore a helmet to stay safe"，答案為安全帽。

3. About how long did it take Lily to learn to ride without help?
   A) Ten minutes  B) One hour  C) About thirty minutes  D) A whole day
   **答案：C**
   解析：文中 "It took her about thirty minutes to learn how to ride without help"，約三十分鐘。

4. Who also wants to learn to ride a bicycle?
   A) Lily's dad  B) Lily's teacher  C) Tim, Lily's little brother  D) Lily's classmate
   **答案：C**
   解析：文中 Tim 說 "I want to learn too! Can you teach me, Lily?"，可知是弟弟 Tim。

5. How does Lily get to school now?
   A) By bus  B) By car  C) She walks  D) She rides her bicycle
   **答案：D**
   解析：最後一段提到 "Now Lily rides her bicycle to school every day"，她現在每天騎自行車上學。

## 文法 (Grammar) — 5題

1. At first, Lily ___ riding without training wheels.
   A) was used to  B) wasn't used to  C) used to  D) didn't used to
   **答案：B**
   解析：「be used to + V-ing」表示習慣於...，否定用 wasn't used to，故選 B。

2. After practicing, Lily felt ___ than before.
   A) confident  B) more confident  C) most confident  D) confidenter
   **答案：B**
   解析：confident 為多音節形容詞，比較級須用 more + 形容詞，故選 more confident。

3. It ___ her about thirty minutes to learn how to ride.
   A) take  B) took  C) taking  D) to take
   **答案：B**
   解析：「It takes + 人 + 時間 + to V」描述過去發生的事，動詞用過去式 took。

4. Riding a bike is ___ than walking.
   A) exciting  B) excited  C) more exciting  D) most exciting
   **答案：C**
   解析：exciting 為多音節形容詞，比較級用 more exciting，表示「比...更令人興奮」。

5. Lily became braver and ___ on busy paths.
   A) more careful  B) careful  C) carefuller  D) most careful
   **答案：A**
   解析：careful 為多音節形容詞，比較級須加 more，故選 more careful，與 braver 並列。

## 單字 (Vocabulary) — 5題

1. If you want to stay safe when riding a bike, you should wear a ___.
   A) helmet  B) jacket  C) scarf  D) backpack
   **答案：A**
   解析：helmet 意思是「安全帽」，騎車時應戴上以保護頭部。

2. Lily felt ___ before she learned to ride, but calm after.
   A) confident  B) nervous  C) proud  D) patient
   **答案：B**
   解析：nervous 意思是「緊張的」，符合她一開始害怕的心情。

3. The opposite of "give up" is ___.
   A) keep trying  B) fall down  C) feel nervous  D) ride slowly
   **答案：A**
   解析：give up 意思是「放棄」，其反義為「keep trying 繼續嘗試」。

4. Lily's dad always ___ her to try new things.
   A) encourages  B) ignores  C) forgets  D) blames
   **答案：A**
   解析：encourage 意思是「鼓勵」，符合爸爸支持她嘗試新事物的行為。

5. Her English has ___ a lot, meaning it has become better.
   A) disappeared  B) improved  C) stopped  D) worried
   **答案：B**
   解析：improve 意思是「進步、改善」，符合「變得更好」的意思。
