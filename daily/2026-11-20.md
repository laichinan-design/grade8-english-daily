## 短文：A Trip to the Aquarium

On Friday morning, Mr. Chen's class went to the city aquarium for a field trip. Mia and her best friend Leo were very excited because they love sea animals.

When they arrived, they walked straight to the shark tank. "Wow, that shark is bigger than my dad's car!" Leo said. Mia laughed and said, "I think the dolphins are more interesting than the sharks. Let's go see the dolphin show next."

The dolphins jumped high and splashed water on the front rows. Everyone got a little wet, but nobody was angry. It was too much fun to be upset.

After the show, the class visited the gift shop. Mia wanted to buy a stuffed penguin, but it was too expensive for her to buy. She chose a small shark keychain instead. Leo bought a postcard with a picture of a jellyfish on it.

By three o'clock, everyone was tired from all the walking. "I'm too tired to walk anymore," said Mia. Their teacher smiled and said, "Don't worry. The bus is waiting outside. You should rest on the bus."

On the way back to school, Leo looked out the window and said, "This was the best field trip of the year!" Mia nodded. "I agree. The aquarium was more amazing than I expected. I can't wait to tell my parents about the sharks and dolphins!"

That night, Mia wrote in her diary, "Today I learned that sea animals are smarter than I thought. I hope our class can visit the aquarium again next year."

## 文法重點

1. **比較級 (Comparative adjectives)**：單音節形容詞加 -er，多音節形容詞用 more + 形容詞，後接 than。
   例：*The shark is bigger than my dad's car.* / *The aquarium was more amazing than I expected.*
2. **too + 形容詞 + (for + 人) + to V**：表示「太...以至於不能...」。若句中有動作的對象，須加 for + 人。
   例：*I'm too tired to walk anymore.* / *The penguin was too expensive for her to buy.*
3. **should 表建議**：should + 原形動詞,表示「應該」，語氣較 must 委婉。
   例：*You should rest on the bus.*

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 "Mia and her best friend Leo were very excited because they love sea animals."，故選 B。
2. Leo 說 "that shark is bigger than my dad's car!"，故選 B。
3. 文中提到企鵝玩偶「too expensive for her to buy」，所以她選了鑰匙圈，故選 B。
4. 老師說 "You should rest on the bus."，故選 C。
5. 日記寫著 "sea animals are smarter than I thought"，故選 B。

**文法**
1. 單音節形容詞 big 的比較級為 bigger，後接 than，故選 B。
2. 多音節形容詞 interesting 的比較級須用 more + 形容詞 + than，故選 B。
3. too + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to V，表示「對某人來說太...而不能」，故選 A。
4. too + 形容詞 + to + 動詞，表示「太...而不能」，故選 A。
5. should 為助動詞,後面須接動詞原形,故選 A。

**單字**
1. aquarium 意為「水族館」，故選 A。
2. 句子為過去式敘述，動詞用過去式 splashed，故選 B。
3. expensive 意為「昂貴的」，故選 B。
4. jellyfish 意為「水母」，符合題目描述，故選 C。
5. field trip 意為「校外教學」，故選 A。
