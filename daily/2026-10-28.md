# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-28

**標題：** A Surprise Birthday Party for Mom
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

On Friday afternoon, David and his sister Amy were planning a surprise party for their mother's birthday.

"Mom's birthday is this Saturday. We should plan something special," David said.

"I agree. I have never baked a cake before, but I want to try this time," Amy said excitedly.

"If we start now, we will have enough time to decorate the living room too," David replied.

"Good idea! We should also invite Grandma and Uncle Mark," Amy added. "They will be so happy to join us."

"Don't forget the balloons. If Dad buys them after work, we can hang them before Mom gets home," David said.

"I'll call Dad right now," Amy said, picking up her phone.

That evening, David wrapped a small gift while Amy carefully frosted the cake.

"It looks delicious! You should feel proud of yourself," David said, smiling.

"Thanks! I have learned a lot from watching cooking videos," Amy laughed.

On Saturday evening, everyone hid behind the sofa. When Mom opened the door, they shouted, "Surprise! Happy Birthday!"

Mom's eyes filled with tears of joy. "This is the best birthday I have ever had," she said, hugging both of her children tightly.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **現在完成時 (Present Perfect)**：`have/has + 過去分詞`，表示過去到現在的經驗或已完成的動作。
   例：*I have never baked a cake before.* / *This is the best birthday I have ever had.*
2. **第一條件句 (First Conditional)**：`If + 現在簡單時, ... will/can + 原形動詞`，表示未來可能發生的情況。
   例：*If we start now, we will have enough time to decorate the living room.* / *If Dad buys them after work, we can hang them before Mom gets home.*
3. **助動詞 should 表建議**：`should + 原形動詞`，表示「應該」。
   例：*We should plan something special.* / *You should feel proud of yourself.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 David 和 Amy 在策劃媽媽生日的驚喜派對（a surprise party for their mother's birthday）。
2. **B** — Amy 說「I have never baked a cake before, but I want to try this time.」
3. **B** — Amy 說「We should also invite Grandma and Uncle Mark.」
4. **B** — 文中提到 David 包裝一份小禮物，而 Amy 則仔細地為蛋糕上糖霜。
5. **C** — 文中提到媽媽眼眶含淚，並緊緊擁抱兩個孩子，說這是她過得最棒的生日。
6. **A** — 現在完成時為 have + 過去分詞，baked 是 bake 的過去分詞，故選 A。
7. **A** — 第一條件句為「If + 現在簡單時, ... will + 原形動詞」，故選 A。
8. **B** — should 之後直接加原形動詞，不加 to 或其他變化，故選 B。
9. **A** — 現在完成時疑問句為「Have/Has + 主詞 + 過去分詞」，故選 A。
10. **B** — 若句主詞為第三人稱單數，現在簡單時動詞要加 -s，故選 B。
11. **A** — decorate 意為「裝飾」，指布置派對場地。
12. **B** — invite 意為「邀請」，表示請某人前來參加。
13. **B** — 此處 frost 作動詞，意為「在蛋糕上塗糖霜裝飾」。
14. **A** — proud 意為「驕傲的；自豪的」，表示對自己的表現感到滿意。
15. **B** — delicious 意為「美味的」，用來形容食物好吃。
