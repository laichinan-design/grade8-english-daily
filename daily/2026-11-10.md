# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-10

**標題：** Amy Joins the School Talent Show
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Amy has always loved singing, but she had never performed in front of a big audience before. This year, her school decided to hold a talent show, and Amy was invited to join. She felt excited and nervous at the same time.

Amy has practiced every day since the sign-up sheet was posted two weeks ago. It takes her about one hour every evening to practice a new song with her guitar teacher, Mr. Huang. "You have improved a lot," Mr. Huang told her last Friday. "I'm proud of how hard you have worked."

On the day of the show, the gym was decorated with colorful balloons and bright lights. The stage was set up by student volunteers, and a long list of performers was posted near the door. Amy's name was called third, right after a dance group and a magic show.

When Amy walked onto the stage, her hands were shaking a little. But as soon as the music started, she forgot about her nervousness and focused on the song. The audience was moved by her clear voice, and loud applause filled the gym when she finished.

After the show, Amy's parents were waiting for her outside the gym. "We are so proud of you," her mom said, giving her a big hug. Amy smiled and said, "It took a lot of practice, but it was worth every minute." That night, she posted a photo of herself on stage and wrote, "Dreams really can come true if you keep trying."

## 📚 文法重點

1. **被動語態 (be + 過去分詞)**：當動作的承受者作主詞時，用 be + 過去分詞表示「被...」。
   例：*The stage was set up by student volunteers.* / *The gym was decorated with colorful balloons.*
2. **現在完成時 + since/for**：have/has + 過去分詞，表示動作從過去持續到現在；since + 時間點，for + 一段時間。
   例：*Amy has practiced every day since the sign-up sheet was posted.*
3. **It takes + 人 + 時間 + to V**：表示「某人做某事需要花費...時間」。
   例：*It takes her about one hour every evening to practice a new song.* / *It took a lot of practice, but it was worth every minute.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **A** — 文中提到 Amy had never performed in front of a big audience before，這是她第一次在大型觀眾前表演，所以既興奮又緊張。
2. **B** — 文中提到 Amy has practiced every day since the sign-up sheet was posted two weeks ago，她從兩週前報名表貼出後就每天練習。
3. **C** — 文中提到 the stage was set up by student volunteers，舞台是由學生志工搭建的。
4. **B** — 文中提到 as soon as the music started, she forgot about her nervousness and focused on the song。
5. **B** — 文中結尾提到 Dreams really can come true if you keep trying，表示只要堅持努力，夢想就能實現。
6. **B** — 舞台是被搭建的（被動），故用 be + 過去分詞 was set up。
7. **C** — 搭配 since + 時間點，須用現在完成時 has practiced，表示持續到現在的動作。
8. **A** — It takes + 人 + 時間 + to V 為固定句型，主詞 It 為第三人稱單數，故用 takes。
9. **C** — 體育館是被裝飾的（被動），故用 be + 過去分詞 was decorated。
10. **B** — since 後接過去某個時間點（the sign-up sheet was posted），表示動作從那時持續至今。
11. **B** — perform 意為「表演」，指在他人面前展現才藝，如唱歌或跳舞。
12. **B** — nervous 意為「緊張的」，指感到擔心或有點害怕。
13. **A** — applause 意為「掌聲」，指表示喜歡或讚賞而發出的拍手聲。
14. **B** — focus on 意為「專注於」，指將注意力完全放在某事上。
15. **B** — worth 意為「值得的」，指某事有足夠的價值值得付出努力。
