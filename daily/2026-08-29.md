## 📖 短文：A Night at the Night Market

Last Friday, my family and I went to the night market near our house. It was very crowded, but everyone looked happy. My little sister, Amy, had never visited a night market before, so she was very excited.

We walked past many stalls. Some sold fried chicken, and others sold sweet desserts. My dad said, "Let's try the stinky tofu stall. I have heard it's the most popular stall here." We waited in line for ten minutes because so many people wanted to try it.

When we finally got our food, Amy said, "This is spicier than I expected!" I laughed and told her to drink some juice. My mom found a fruit stall and bought some fresh mangoes. The seller gave us a good bargain because we bought a lot.

After dinner, we looked for desserts. I have eaten shaved ice many times, but this shop's mango shaved ice was the best I have ever tasted. Amy agreed that it was more delicious than the ice cream she usually has at home.

Before we left, Dad checked his phone and read some reviews about a new drink stall. "People say the price is a little high, but the drinks taste amazing," he said. We decided to try it next time.

On the way home, Amy said, "This was the most fun night I've had this month! Can we come back next week?" I smiled and said, "I definitely think we should. I really recommend this night market to everyone who loves food."

## 📐 文法重點（本篇 2 個文法概念）

1. **現在完成式（Present Perfect: have/has + p.p.）**：表示「過去到現在的經驗或已完成的動作」。
   例：*had never visited*、*I have heard*、*I have eaten...many times*、*the best I have ever tasted*。
2. **比較級與最高級（Comparative & Superlative）**：
   - 比較級：形容詞 + er / more + 形容詞 + than → *spicier than*、*more delicious than*
   - 最高級：the + 形容詞 + est / the most + 形容詞 → *the most popular*、*the most fun*

## ✅ 答案 Key（作答後再看！）

**1. (B)** — 文中提到 "had never visited a night market before"，所以她很興奮。
**2. (C)** — Dad 說 "I have heard it's the most popular stall here."
**3. (C)** — 文中 "The seller gave us a good bargain because we bought a lot."（因為買很多）
**4. (B)** — "it was more delicious than the ice cream she usually has at home." 用比較級 more delicious than。
**5. (B)** — "Dad checked his phone and read some reviews about a new drink stall."
**6. (B)** — 過去某時間點之前已完成的經驗，用「過去完成式 had + p.p.」：had never visited。
**7. (C)** — spicy 為短母音結尾雙寫變化字，比較級為 spicier（去 y 加 ier），故選 (C)。
**8. (C)** — 最高級用 the most + 形容詞：the most delicious。(D) more...than 用法錯誤，且與最高級句型不合。
**9. (B)** — bargain 在此指「划算、便宜的交易」，因為買很多所以老闆算便宜。
**10. (B)** — eat 的過去分詞是 eaten，現在完成式為 have/has + p.p.，主詞 I 搭配 have，故正解為 have eaten。
