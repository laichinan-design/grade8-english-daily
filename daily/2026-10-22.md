# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-22

**標題：A School Bake Sale for Charity**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文 (Passage)

Lily's class decided to hold a bake sale to raise money for a children's hospital. On Saturday morning, the students set up a table in front of the school gate. Baking cookies and cupcakes took a lot of time, but everyone worked together happily.

"Would you like to try our chocolate cupcakes?" Lily asked a customer with a bright smile. The woman nodded and bought three.

As soon as the bell rang at nine o'clock, more customers arrived. Some wanted sweet treats; others preferred something healthy, like fruit tarts. Ben, the class leader, let his classmates take turns selling and counting money.

By noon, the table was almost empty. "We should save some for the teachers," Mrs. Chen reminded them with a laugh.

Volunteering for a good cause made everyone feel proud. Lily's teacher didn't make the students donate every cent, but most of them chose to give all the money to the hospital. Collecting donations like this, they realized, could change someone's life.

In the end, the bake sale raised more than five thousand dollars. "I'm amazed at how generous you all are," the principal said during the closing ceremony. Lily smiled, thinking about the sick children who would soon receive new toys and books because of their small kindness.

## 文法重點

1. **動名詞 (V-ing) 作主詞或受詞**：動詞加 -ing 可當名詞使用，放在句首作主詞時，動詞需用單數形式，如 *Baking cookies and cupcakes took a lot of time.* / *Volunteering for a good cause made everyone feel proud.*
2. **as soon as + 子句**：表示「一...就...」，連接兩個幾乎同時發生的動作，如 *As soon as the bell rang at nine o'clock, more customers arrived.*
3. **使役動詞 let / make + 受詞 + 原形動詞**：let 表示「允許/讓某人做某事」，make 表示「強迫某人做某事」，否定用 didn't make，如 *Ben let his classmates take turns selling and counting money.* / *Lily's teacher didn't make the students donate every cent.*

## 閱讀理解 (Reading) — 5題

1. What was the purpose of the bake sale?
   A) To celebrate the school's anniversary  B) To raise money for a children's hospital  C) To sell old textbooks  D) To collect toys for teachers
   **答案：B**
   解析：文中提到他們舉辦義賣是「to raise money for a children's hospital」，所以答案是 B。

2. When did more customers start arriving?
   A) Right after breakfast  B) As soon as the bell rang at nine o'clock  C) Before the bake sale started  D) At noon
   **答案：B**
   解析：文中寫道「As soon as the bell rang at nine o'clock, more customers arrived.」，所以答案是 B。

3. What did Ben let his classmates do?
   A) Go home early  B) Take turns selling and counting money  C) Eat all the cupcakes  D) Skip the bake sale
   **答案：B**
   解析：文中提到「Ben...let his classmates take turns selling and counting money.」，所以答案是 B。

4. How much money did the bake sale raise in total?
   A) Less than one thousand dollars  B) About three thousand dollars  C) More than five thousand dollars  D) Exactly five thousand dollars
   **答案：C**
   解析：文中提到「the bake sale raised more than five thousand dollars.」，所以答案是 C。

5. How did the principal feel about the students?
   A) Angry because they made a mess  B) Amazed at how generous they were  C) Worried about the money  D) Disappointed with the sales
   **答案：B**
   解析：校長說「I'm amazed at how generous you all are」，所以答案是 B。

## 文法 (Grammar) — 5題

1. ___ cookies and cupcakes took a lot of time, but everyone worked together happily.
   A) Bake  B) Baked  C) Baking  D) To baking
   **答案：C**
   解析：動名詞 Baking 可作句子主詞，表示「烘焙這件事」，故選 C。

2. ___ the bell rang at nine o'clock, more customers arrived.
   A) As soon as  B) Although  C) Because of  D) In order to
   **答案：A**
   解析：as soon as 表示「一...就...」，符合句意，故選 A。

3. Ben ___ his classmates take turns selling and counting money.
   A) made  B) let  C) hope  D) wanted
   **答案：B**
   解析：let + 受詞 + 原形動詞，表示「允許/讓某人做某事」，故選 B。

4. Lily's teacher didn't ___ the students donate every cent.
   A) make  B) let him  C) making  D) to make
   **答案：A**
   解析：didn't make + 受詞 + 原形動詞，表示「沒有強迫某人做某事」，故選 A。

5. ___ for a good cause made everyone feel proud.
   A) Volunteer  B) Volunteers  C) Volunteering  D) To volunteered
   **答案：C**
   解析：動名詞片語 Volunteering 作主詞，後面動詞用單數 made，故選 C。
