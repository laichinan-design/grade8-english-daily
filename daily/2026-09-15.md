# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-15

**主題：A Weekend at the Night Market（夜市週末）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Every Friday night, Uncle Wang's family visits the busy night market near their apartment. Last weekend, Amy and her cousin Ben decided to explore the market together. The night market was more crowded than usual because a music festival was happening nearby.

Amy wanted to try the stinky tofu, but Ben thought it smelled too strange to eat. "Just try one bite," Amy said with a laugh. "It's delicious once you get used to it." Ben finally agreed, and to his surprise, he liked it more than he expected.

They also stopped at a drink stand and ordered two cups of bubble tea. The line was so long that they had to wait almost twenty minutes. While waiting, Ben noticed a stall selling handmade bracelets. He picked the cheapest one, but Amy chose the most colorful bracelet on the table.

Near the end of the market, they played a ring toss game. Amy was better at the game than Ben, and she won a small stuffed animal. "You're the luckiest person here tonight!" Ben joked.

Before heading home, they shared a plate of grilled squid. It was hot, but not too spicy for them to finish. Amy said the night market trip was the best part of her whole week. Ben agreed and promised to bring his little sister next time so she could try the delicious snacks too.

## 文法重點

1. **比較級與最高級 (Comparative & Superlative Adjectives)**：兩者比較用「形容詞-er / more + 形容詞 + than」，三者以上比較用「the + 形容詞-est / the most + 形容詞」。例如：*The night market was more crowded than usual.*；*Amy was better at the game than Ben.*；*Amy chose the most colorful bracelet.*；*You're the luckiest person here tonight!*
2. **too...to / so...that 句型**：「too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞」表示「太…以致於不能…」；「so + 形容詞 + that + 子句」表示「太…以致於…」。例如：*It smelled too strange to eat.*；*The line was so long that they had to wait almost twenty minutes.*；*It was not too spicy for them to finish.*

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 The night market was more crowded than usual because a music festival was happening nearby，答案是 B。
2. 文中寫道 Ben thought it smelled too strange to eat，答案是 A。
3. 文中提到 they had to wait almost twenty minutes，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 she won a small stuffed animal，答案是 C。
5. 文末提到 Ben agreed and promised to bring his little sister next time，答案是 A。

**文法題**
1. 兩者比較（平常 vs. 上週末）用 more + 形容詞 + than，故選 more crowded。
2. so + 形容詞 + that + 子句 表示「太…以致於…」，故選 so。
3. 三者以上比較選出最…的，須用 the most + 形容詞，故選 the most。
4. 兩者比較用比較級 better + than，故選 better。
5. too + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to + 原形動詞，表示「對某人來說太…而不能…」，故選 for。

**單字題**
1. crowded 意指「擁擠的」，符合句意「有太多人的地方」。
2. explore 意指「探索」，符合句意「四處遊覽並發現新地方」。
3. stand 指「攤位」，符合句意「賣飲料的攤位」。
4. handmade 意指「手工製作的」，符合句意。
5. stuffed animal 指「填充玩偶」，符合句意「填充棉花的軟玩具」。
