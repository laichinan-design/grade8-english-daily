## Lucy Teaches Grandma to Video Call
國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

### 短文

Lucy's grandmother, who lives in a small town, missed her family very much because they lived far away. One day, Lucy decided to teach her grandmother how to use a smartphone to make video calls. At first, Grandma was confused and said she wasn't used to touching a screen. "I used to write letters instead," she laughed. Lucy patiently showed her step by step. She explained that it would take only a few minutes to learn, and that video calls were much faster than letters. Lucy opened the app, which had a simple design, and tapped the green button to start a call with her uncle, who was studying abroad. When her uncle's face appeared on the screen, Grandma was so excited that she almost dropped the phone! "I can really see you!" she said happily. They talked for almost an hour, sharing news about the weather, the garden, and even the neighbor's noisy dog. After the call ended, Grandma told Lucy that she was getting used to the new technology already. "Maybe I'll even learn to send photos next," she said with a smile. Lucy felt proud that she had helped her grandmother connect with family members who lived so far away. From then on, Grandma video-called her children and grandchildren every weekend, and she never felt lonely again.

### 文法重點

1. **used to + 原形動詞（過去的習慣）vs. be/get used to + V-ing（習慣於）**：used to + 原形動詞表示「過去常常做、但現在已不再如此」的習慣；be/get used to + V-ing（或名詞）表示「習慣於...」。
   例：I used to write letters instead.（過去習慣寫信）/ Grandma was getting used to the new technology.（逐漸習慣新科技）
2. **關係代詞 who / which 引導形容詞子句**：who 代替「人」，which 代替「事物」，用來修飾前面的名詞並補充說明。
   例：Lucy's grandmother, who lives in a small town, missed her family. / Lucy opened the app, which had a simple design.
3. **It takes + (某人) + 時間 + to V**：表示「做某事需要花...時間」。
   例：It would take only a few minutes to learn.

### 答案 Key

**閱讀理解**：1-A　2-B　3-B　4-B　5-B
**文法**：1-A　2-B　3-B　4-C　5-C
**單字**：1-C　2-B　3-B　4-C　5-B

#### 解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中說奶奶住在小鎮，因為家人住得很遠，所以她很想念他們。
2. 奶奶說「I used to write letters instead」，表示過去習慣寫信聯絡。
3. 文中提到 Lucy 打給在國外讀書的舅舅/叔叔，讓奶奶看看視訊通話怎麼運作。
4. 文中提到奶奶看到螢幕上出現姪子的臉時非常興奮，差點把手機弄掉。
5. 奶奶說「Maybe I'll even learn to send photos next」，表示她接下來也許會學傳照片。

**文法**
1. be used to + V-ing 表示「習慣於」做某事，故選 used to。
2. used to + 原形動詞表示過去的習慣，現在已不再如此，故選 used to。
3. who 是代替「人」的關係代詞，用來引導形容詞子句修飾 grandmother。
4. which 是代替「事物」的關係代詞，用來引導形容詞子句修飾 app。
5. It takes + 時間 + to V 表示「做某事需要花...時間」，故選 to learn。

**單字**
1. confused 意為「困惑的」，指不明白某事而感到疑惑。
2. patiently 意為「有耐心地」，指不急躁、不抱怨地做某事。
3. abroad 意為「在國外」。
4. appear 意為「出現」，指開始被看見。
5. lonely 意為「孤單的」，指因獨自一人而感到難過。
