# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-14

**主題：A School Bake Sale（學校烘焙義賣）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last Saturday, our class held a bake sale to raise money for a local charity. Mia and her brother Leo decided to volunteer early in the morning. They followed a simple recipe to make chocolate cookies and decorate cupcakes with colorful icing. "Baking cookies is fun, but it takes a lot of time," Mia said with a smile.

By nine o'clock, the tables were covered with delicious snacks. Price tags were placed next to every item, and Ms. Chen helped the students set up a donation box. Soon, customers started to arrive. Parents, teachers, and even the school principal came to buy something sweet.

Leo felt a little nervous at first, but he became more confident after selling his first plate of cookies. "Look! Our cupcakes were sold in ten minutes!" he shouted happily. Mia enjoyed decorating the desserts and talking with customers about the recipe.

By noon, most of the food was gone. Only a few leftover cookies remained on the table. "We should feel proud of ourselves," Ms. Chen said. "All the money will be donated to children who need school supplies."

The students carefully counted the coins and bills. In total, they collected more than five thousand dollars! Everyone cheered because the bake sale was such a big success. Mia and Leo learned that helping others can also be a lot of fun. Next year, they hope to make the bake sale even more popular by inviting more classes to join.

## 文法重點

1. **被動語態 (Passive Voice: be + 過去分詞)**：表示動作的接受者，強調「被做了什麼」而非誰做的。例如：*The cookies were baked by Mia.*；*All the money will be donated to children who need school supplies.*；*Price tags were placed next to every item.*
2. **動名詞作主詞/受詞 (Gerund as Subject/Object)**：V-ing 可當名詞使用，放在句首當主詞，或放在動詞後面當受詞。例如：*Baking cookies is fun.*；*Mia enjoyed decorating the desserts.*；*Leo enjoys selling cookies to customers.*

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 短文開頭提到 they held a bake sale to raise money for a local charity，所以答案是 B。
2. 文中提到 Ms. Chen helped the students set up a donation box，答案是 B。
3. 文中寫道 Leo felt a little nervous at first，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 they collected more than five thousand dollars，答案是 C。
5. 文末提到 they hope to make the bake sale even more popular by inviting more classes to join，答案是 B。

**文法題**
1. 被動語態為 be動詞 + 過去分詞，主詞是動作的接受者（cookies），故選 B。
2. 動名詞(V-ing)可放句首當主詞，故選 Decorating。
3. money 是「被捐贈」的對象，須用未來式被動語態 will be + 過去分詞，故選 D。
4. enjoy 後面須接動名詞(V-ing)作受詞，故選 selling。
5. 標籤「被放置」，須用過去式被動語態 was/were + 過去分詞，故選 C。

**單字題**
1. volunteer 意指「自願做某事、當志工」，符合句意。
2. delicious 意指「美味的」，用來形容食物味道很好。
3. leftover 指「剩下的（食物）」，符合句意「剩下的點心」。
4. proud 意指「驕傲的、感到自豪的」，符合句意。
5. customer 指「顧客」，符合句意「排隊等候買餅乾的顧客」。
