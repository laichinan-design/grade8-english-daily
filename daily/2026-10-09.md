# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-09

**標題：Planning a Weekend Camping Trip**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Jack and his cousin Lily were planning a camping trip for the coming weekend. They sat at the kitchen table, making a list of things to bring.

"If the weather is good, we can set up the tent by the lake," Jack said, checking his phone for the forecast.

"What if it rains?" Lily asked. "Should we still go?"

"If it rains on Saturday, we'll wait until Sunday," Jack answered. "The forecast says it probably won't rain, though."

"Great! Then we need to decide what to bring," Lily said. She wrote down a list: a tent, sleeping bags, flashlights, and enough food for two days.

"Don't forget bug spray," Jack added. "Last time, the mosquitoes were terrible."

"Good idea. I'll also pack a warm jacket," Lily said. "Is it warm enough to swim in the lake?"

"I'm not sure," Jack said. "The water might still be too cold this time of year. We should check before we jump in."

On Saturday morning, the sky was clear and sunny. Jack and Lily drove to the campsite with their parents. They set up the tent quickly and spent the afternoon hiking around the lake. That night, they sat by the campfire, roasted marshmallows, and told funny stories under the stars.

"This is the best camping trip ever," Lily said with a smile.

"I agree," Jack said. "Let's come back again next summer."

## 文法重點

1. **第一類條件句 (First Conditional)**：If + 現在簡單式, ... will + 原形動詞，表示條件成立時可能發生的結果，如 *If it rains on Saturday, we'll wait until Sunday.*
2. **疑問詞 + to + 原形動詞**：what to / how to 等用來表示「該做什麼/該怎麼做」，如 *We need to decide what to bring.*
3. **enough 的用法**：形容詞/副詞 + enough (+ to + 原形動詞)，enough 放在被修飾的形容詞或副詞之後，如 *Is it warm enough to swim in the lake?*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **A** — 文中提到他們正在計畫一場露營旅行。
2. **C** — Jack 說「If it rains on Saturday, we'll wait until Sunday」。
3. **B** — Jack 說上次蚊子很可怕，所以提醒要帶防蟲噴霧。
4. **C** — Jack 說這個季節湖水可能還是太冷。
5. **B** — 文中提到他們晚上坐在營火旁烤棉花糖、說故事。

### 文法
1. **B (rains)** — 第一類條件句的 if 子句要用現在簡單式，主詞 it 第三人稱單數動詞加 s。
2. **A (to)** — what to + 原形動詞表示「該帶什麼」。
3. **C (enough)** — enough 需放在形容詞 warm 之後，構成 warm enough to V。
4. **C (making)** — 此處用現在分詞表示與主要動作同時發生的伴隨動作。
5. **A (before)** — before 引導時間子句，表示「在...之前」。

### 單字
1. **B** — forecast 指「(天氣)預報」。
2. **A** — tent 指「帳篷」。
3. **B** — mosquito 指「蚊子」。
4. **B** — roast 指「烘烤」，如烤棉花糖。
5. **A** — campsite 指「露營地」。
