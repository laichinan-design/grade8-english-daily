# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-25

**主題：A Field Trip to the Science Museum（科學博物館校外教學）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Next Friday, Mr. Chen's class is going to visit the National Science Museum. The students are very excited because they will see many interesting exhibits about robots and space.

"You have to bring a notebook and a pencil," Mr. Chen reminded the class. "You must also stay with your group at all times."

On the morning of the trip, everyone arrived at school early. When they got to the museum, a guide named Ms. Wu welcomed them. "This museum was built ten years ago," she explained. "Many of the machines here were designed by scientists from all over the world."

The students walked through the space hall first. They saw a real rocket model and learned how astronauts live in space. Then they visited the robot hall, where a friendly robot arm was demonstrated by an engineer. "Wow, that's amazing!" shouted Tom. "Can I try it myself?"

"Sure," said the engineer. "But you have to wait for your turn."

After lunch, the class watched a short film about the future of technology. "In twenty years, many jobs will be done by robots," the film explained. Some students looked worried, but others thought it sounded exciting.

Before leaving, Mr. Chen said, "I hope you learned something new today. Next month, we are going to visit an art museum instead." Everyone cheered happily, already looking forward to the next trip.

## 文法重點

1. 未來式 (Future Tense: be going to / will)：be going to 常用於「已計畫好的事」或「有跡象顯示會發生的事」；will 常用於「當下臨時決定」或「預測」。例句：Mr. Chen's class is going to visit the National Science Museum.；In twenty years, many jobs will be done by robots.
2. 情態動詞 have to / must（義務）：表示「必須」，have to 語氣較中性（外在規定），must 語氣較強（說話者本身的要求）。例句：You have to bring a notebook and a pencil.；You must also stay with your group at all times.
3. 被動語態 (Passive Voice: be + p.p.)：當動作的執行者不重要或強調「被...如何」時使用，句型為 be 動詞 + 過去分詞，常與 by 表示動作執行者。例句：This museum was built ten years ago.；Many of the machines here were designed by scientists from all over the world.

## 答案 Key

**閱讀理解**：1-A　2-D　3-B　4-C　5-C

**單字題**：1-C　2-A　3-D　4-B　5-C

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Mr. Chen's class is going to visit the National Science Museum，答案是科學博物館，故選 A。
2. 文中提到 You have to bring a notebook and a pencil，答案是筆記本和鉛筆，故選 D。
3. 文中提到 Many of the machines here were designed by scientists from all over the world，答案是來自世界各地的科學家，故選 B。
4. 文中提到 The students walked through the space hall first. They saw a real rocket model，答案是太空展區的火箭模型，故選 C。
5. 文中提到 Next month, we are going to visit an art museum instead，答案是美術館，故選 C。

**文法題**
1. 文中直接引用 we are going to visit an art museum，表示已經計畫好的事，用 be going to，故答案為 A。
2. 文中 You must also stay with your group at all times，must 表示說話者強烈要求「必須」，故答案為 C。
3. 博物館是「被建造」的，須用被動語態 be + p.p.，故答案為 B（was built）。
4. 工作是「被完成」的，未來式被動語態為 will be + p.p.，故答案為 C（will be done）。
5. 此句表示「當下臨時決定」要做的動作，須用 will，故答案為 A。

**單字題**
1. guide 意思是「導覽員；嚮導」，符合帶領大家參觀的角色，故答案為 C（guide）。
2. demonstrate 意思是「示範；展示」，符合工程師展示機械手臂的情境，故答案為 A（demonstrated）。
3. astronaut 意思是「太空人」，故答案為 D（astronaut）。
4. amazing 意思是「令人驚奇的」，符合看到有趣事物時的驚嘆語氣，故答案為 B（amazing）。
5. worried 意思是「擔心的」，故答案為 C（worried）。
