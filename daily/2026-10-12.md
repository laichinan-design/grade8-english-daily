# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-12

**標題：Ordering Food at a Restaurant**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Peter and his mother were having dinner at Sunny Diner on Saturday night. The restaurant was busy, and they had to wait for a table.

"What would you like to eat tonight?" Mom asked, looking at the menu.

"I'd like to try the spaghetti," Peter said. "It looks more delicious than the fried rice."

"Good choice. I'd like to order the grilled chicken salad," Mom said. "It's healthier than the burger."

When the waiter came, Mom asked, "Could we also have some orange juice, please?"

"Sure," the waiter said with a smile. "Would you like anything else?"

"No, thank you. That's all for now," Mom said.

After a few minutes, the food arrived. The spaghetti was too hot to eat right away, so Peter waited and blew on it gently.

"Be careful," Mom reminded him. "Don't burn your tongue."

"This spaghetti tastes even better than the one at school," Peter said happily. "Can we come back next weekend?"

"Of course," Mom laughed. "If you finish your vegetables tonight, we can come back next Saturday."

Peter smiled and quickly ate his salad. The dinner was delicious, and they both felt full and happy. Before leaving, Peter thanked the waiter politely and said it was the best meal he had had in weeks.

## 文法重點

1. **「Would like + to V」／「I'd like to...」**：比 want 更委婉禮貌地表達「我想要」或提出請求，如 *I'd like to try the spaghetti.* / *Would you like anything else?*
2. **形容詞比較級**：單音節形容詞加 -er，多音節形容詞前加 more，用來比較兩者的差異，如 *more delicious than*、*healthier than*、*even better than*。
3. **「too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞」**：表示「太...而不能...」，如 *The spaghetti was too hot to eat right away.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **A** — 文章第一句提到他們星期六晚上在 Sunny Diner 吃晚餐。
2. **B** — Peter 說「I'd like to try the spaghetti.」，所以他點了義大利麵。
3. **C** — Mom 說她要點 grilled chicken salad（烤雞沙拉）。
4. **B** — 文中提到 the spaghetti was too hot to eat right away，所以他先等待並輕輕吹涼。
5. **B** — Mom 說「If you finish your vegetables tonight, we can come back next Saturday.」

### 文法
1. **B** — would like 後面要接「to + 原形動詞」，表示委婉地想要做某事。
2. **A (more delicious)** — delicious 是多音節形容詞，比較級要用 more + 形容詞。
3. **B (too hot to drink)** — 「too + 形容詞 + to + 原形動詞」表示「太...而不能...」。
4. **B (healthier)** — 形容詞字尾為「子音+y」時，比較級要去 y 加 ier，healthy → healthier。
5. **B (I'd like to order a salad.)** — 「I'd like to + 原形動詞」是禮貌地表達「我想要」的正確句型。

### 單字
1. **B** — menu 指「菜單」，顧客用它來選擇餐點。
2. **B** — politely 是副詞，意思是「禮貌地」。
3. **B (gently)** — 修飾動詞 blew 需用副詞 gently（溫和地），而非形容詞 gentle。
4. **B** — remind 指「提醒」。
5. **B** — arrive 指「到達」。
