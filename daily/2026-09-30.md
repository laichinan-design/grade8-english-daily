# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-30

**主題：Mia's First Library Card（Mia 的第一張借書證）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

On Saturday morning, Mia visited the public library near her school for the first time. She wanted to borrow some story books for the weekend, but she didn't have a library card yet.

At the front desk, a friendly librarian named Ms. Chen greeted her. "Welcome! You have to fill out this form if you want a library card," Ms. Chen said.

Mia filled in her name, address, and phone number carefully. She also had to show her student ID card. "It's easier than I thought," Mia said with a smile.

After she got her card, Mia walked around the shelves. The library was much quieter than the classroom, and the shelves were more organized than she expected. She found a science book that looked more interesting than the novel she had picked first.

"Excuse me," Mia asked a librarian who was pushing a cart of books, "how many books can I borrow at one time?"

"You can borrow up to five books," the librarian answered, "but you must return them within two weeks."

Mia chose three books that she really wanted to read: a mystery story, a science book, and a comic book. Before she left, she checked the due date on the receipt so she wouldn't forget.

On her way home, Mia felt proud of herself. She realized that visiting the library could be more fun than she had imagined. She promised herself that she would come back every weekend to borrow new books.

## 文法重點

1. must / have to 表義務（必須）：must 與 have to 都表示「必須」做某事，have to 會依主詞做動詞變化（has to），must 則不隨主詞變化。例句：You have to fill out this form if you want a library card. / You must return them within two weeks.
2. 比較級 (-er than / more...than)：形容詞比較級用來比較兩者的差異，短音節形容詞字尾加 -er than，長音節形容詞前面加 more，後面加 than。例句：The library was much quieter than the classroom. / The shelves were more organized than she expected.
3. 關係代名詞 who / that：who 用來引導形容詞子句修飾「人」，that（或 which）用來修飾「事物」，說明先行詞的補充資訊。例句：Mia asked a librarian who was pushing a cart of books. / She found a science book that looked more interesting than the novel.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 "She wanted to borrow some story books for the weekend"，故答案為 B。
2. 文中寫道 "She also had to show her student ID card."，故答案為 B。
3. 文中提到 "The library was much quieter than the classroom, and the shelves were more organized than she expected."，故答案為 B。
4. 文中寫道 "You can borrow up to five books... but you must return them within two weeks."，故答案為 B。
5. 文末提到 "She promised herself that she would come back every weekend to borrow new books."，故答案為 C。

**文法題**
1. 主詞是 You，have to 表示「必須」，不隨第三人稱變化為 has to，故答案為 A。
2. quieter 是比較級形容詞，比較級後面要接 than，故答案為 B。
3. more organized 是比較級用法（more + 形容詞），後面要接 than，故答案為 A。
4. 先行詞 a librarian 是「人」，關係代名詞在子句中做主詞，應用 who，故答案為 B。
5. 先行詞 a science book 是「事物」，關係代名詞在子句中做主詞，應用 that（或 which），故答案為 C。

**單字題**
1. borrow 意思是「借」，指拿走某物並計畫之後歸還，故答案為 B。
2. librarian 意思是「圖書館員」，在圖書館工作並協助大家找書，故答案為 B。
3. organized 意思是「整齊有序的」，指排列得有條理，故答案為 B。
4. due date 意思是「到期日」，指必須歸還或完成的特定日期，故答案為 B。
5. proud 意思是「驕傲的」，指對自己所做的事感到滿意與高興，故答案為 B。
