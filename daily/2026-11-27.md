**標題：Getting Ready for the School Talent Show**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文 Passage

Our school is going to hold a talent show next Friday, and almost every class wants to join. Mr. Wang, our homeroom teacher, told us, "This is going to be the most exciting event of the semester!"

My best friend, Tina, who plays the guitar really well, decided to sing an English song with two classmates. Not only will they sing, but they will also dance during the chorus. They have had a rehearsal every day after school for two weeks.

I don't have much talent for singing or dancing, so I offered to help backstage instead. I'm going to be in charge of the lights and the microphone. Our group leader, who is very organized, made a schedule so that nobody would be late.

Yesterday, something funny happened. During rehearsal, the microphone, which was quite old, suddenly stopped working, and Tina had to finish her song without it. Everyone laughed, but she stayed calm and kept singing bravely. "The show must go on," she said with a smile.

Tonight, we are going to have our final rehearsal before the big day. I feel both nervous and excited. I believe that students who work hard together can create something amazing, no matter how small their roles are.

If everything goes well tomorrow, our class will probably win first prize. Even if we don't win, I think we will still remember this experience, because preparing for the show has brought our class closer together.

## 文法重點 Grammar Points

1. 「be going to」表示事先計畫好的未來動作：be going to + 動詞原形，強調說話前就已經決定好的計畫，be 動詞依主詞變化(am/is/are)。例：We are going to have our final rehearsal tonight. / I'm going to be in charge of the lights.
2. 關係代名詞 who / which（限定關係子句）：who 引導形容詞子句修飾「人」，在子句中做主詞；which 則用來修飾「事物」。例：My best friend, Tina, who plays the guitar really well, decided to sing. / The microphone, which was quite old, suddenly stopped working.
3. not only...but also 句型(對等句型)：連接兩個對等的動作或特質，表示「不僅...而且...」；當 not only 放在句首時，後面的子句需倒裝(助動詞提前)。例：Not only will they sing, but they will also dance during the chorus.

## 閱讀理解 Reading Comprehension (5題)

1. What event is the whole school getting ready for?
   A) A sports day B) A talent show C) A field trip D) A science fair
   解析：文中第一句提到「Our school is going to hold a talent show next Friday」，所以是才藝表演。

2. What job did the narrator choose to do for the show?
   A) Singing with Tina B) Dancing in the chorus C) Helping backstage with lights and the microphone D) Writing the schedule for Mr. Wang
   解析：文中提到「I offered to help backstage instead. I'm going to be in charge of the lights and the microphone.」，答案是負責燈光與麥克風。

3. What problem happened during rehearsal yesterday?
   A) Tina forgot the lyrics B) The microphone stopped working C) The lights went out D) Mr. Wang canceled the rehearsal
   解析：文中寫到「the microphone...suddenly stopped working」，所以是麥克風故障。

4. How did Tina react when the microphone broke?
   A) She cried and left the stage B) She stayed calm and kept singing bravely C) She asked to change the song D) She blamed her classmates
   解析：文中提到「she stayed calm and kept singing bravely」，表示她冷靜且勇敢地繼續唱完。

5. What does the narrator believe about the experience, even if the class doesn't win?
   A) Only winning matters in the end B) Practicing was a waste of time C) They will still remember it because it brought the class closer together D) Next time they should choose a different group leader
   解析：文末提到「we will still remember this experience...because preparing for the show has brought our class closer together」，所以答案是即使沒贏，這段經歷仍有意義。

## 文法 Grammar (5題)

1. We ___ have our final rehearsal tonight before the big show. (事先已計畫好)
   A) have B) are going to have C) having D) will having
   解析：表示事先計劃好的未來動作要用 be going to + 動詞原形，故選 are going to have。

2. Tina, ___ sings and plays the guitar, will perform first.
   A) which B) who C) whom D) whose
   解析：who 用來引導形容詞子句修飾「人」，在子句中做主詞，Tina 是人，故選 who。

3. Choose the sentence that correctly uses "not only...but also".
   A) Not only they will sing, but also they will dance. B) Not only will they sing, but they will also dance. C) Not only sing they will, but also dance. D) Not only they sing, but will also dance.
   解析：not only 放在句首時，後面的子句要倒裝，把助動詞(will)移到主詞前面，故選 B：Not only will they sing, but they will also dance。

4. Choose the correct sentence.
   A) The microphone, who stopped working, was very old. B) The microphone, which stopped working, was very old. C) The microphone, whom stopped working, was very old. D) The microphone, whose stopped working, was very old.
   解析：which 用來引導形容詞子句修飾「事物」，microphone 是物品不是人，故選 which 而非 who。

5. Which sentence best shows a plan that was decided before now, not a sudden decision?
   A) I will help backstage. B) I am going to help backstage. C) I help backstage. D) I helped backstage.
   解析：be going to 強調「事先計畫好」的未來動作，比臨時決定的 will 更適合描述提前安排好的計畫，故選 B。

## 單字 Vocabulary (5題)

1. Which word means "負責"?
   A) rehearsal B) in charge of C) backstage D) nervous
   解析：in charge of 意思是「負責」，例如 in charge of the lights(負責燈光)。

2. The dancers waited ___ before the show began.
   A) chorus B) backstage C) microphone D) semester
   解析：backstage 意思是「後台」，表演者在後台等待上場，故選 backstage。

3. She speaks into the ___ so everyone in the hall can hear her.
   A) chorus B) microphone C) talent D) rehearsal
   解析：microphone 是「麥克風」，用來放大聲音讓大家聽到，故選 microphone。

4. Which word means "勇敢地"?
   A) bravely B) nervous C) organized D) semester
   解析：bravely 是副詞，意思是「勇敢地」，故選 bravely。

5. We have one more ___ before the real performance on Friday.
   A) semester B) rehearsal C) prize D) talent
   解析：rehearsal 意思是「排練」，表演前通常會多次排練，故選 rehearsal。
