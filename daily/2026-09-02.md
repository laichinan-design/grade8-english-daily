# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-02

**主題：Getting Ready for the Talent Show（準備才藝表演）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Next Friday, Chen Wei's class will have a talent show. He and his best friend Lisa are planning to sing a song together on stage. Every afternoon after school, they rehearse in the music room.

"I'm so nervous," Chen Wei said while tuning his guitar. "What if I forget the words?"

"Don't worry," Lisa said with a smile. "If you practice every day, you will remember everything by Friday. You always work hard, so I'm sure we'll do great."

They usually rehearse for an hour, but yesterday they were too tired to finish the whole song. "Let's take a short break," Lisa suggested. "We can try again in ten minutes."

After the break, they practiced the song again. This time, Chen Wei sang confidently enough to make Lisa smile. "See? If you stay calm, you will sound wonderful," she said.

On the day of the show, the auditorium was full of students and teachers. Backstage, Chen Wei's hands were shaking, but Lisa held his hand. "If we make a mistake, we will just keep smiling," she whispered. "The audience won't even notice."

When their names were called, they walked onto the stage. The bright lights were almost too strong to see the crowd, but they heard the loud applause. They took a deep breath and began to sing. Their voices blended perfectly, and everyone clapped along.

After the performance, their teacher praised them. "You two rarely make mistakes, and today you were amazing!" Chen Wei grinned. "If we keep practicing like this, we will join the city competition next year," he said happily.

## 文法重點

1. **第一條件句 (First Conditional)**：「If + 現在簡單式, ... will + 原形動詞」用來表達「有可能發生」的未來條件與結果。例如：*If you practice every day, you will remember everything.*（if 子句用現在式，主要子句用 will + 原形動詞，不可用 will 兩次）。
2. **too + 形容詞 + to V / 形容詞 + enough to V**：「too...to」表示「太…以致於不能…」，帶有負面結果（*they were too tired to finish*）；「...enough to」表示「足夠…以至於能…」，帶有正面結果（*confidently enough to make Lisa smile*）。
3. **頻率副詞 (always/usually/often/rarely/never) 的位置**：放在一般動詞之前、be 動詞之後。例如：*You always work hard.*（work 是一般動詞，always 放其前）；*You two rarely make mistakes.*（rarely 放在一般動詞 make 之前）。

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. B — 文中提到「Chen Wei's class will have a talent show」。
2. B — 文中提到他們「are planning to sing a song together on stage」。
3. A — 文中提到「they were too tired to finish the whole song」。
4. B — 文中提到「Lisa held his hand」並「she whispered」安慰他。
5. B — 文中提到老師說「You two rarely make mistakes, and today you were amazing!」。

**文法**
1. A — 第一條件句：if 子句用現在式，主要子句用 will + 原形動詞，故選 will。
2. A — 「too + 形容詞 + to V」表示「太…以致於不能…」，故選 too。
3. C — 「形容詞/副詞 + enough + to V」表示「足夠…以至於能…」，enough 需放在副詞之後，故選 enough。
4. B — 頻率副詞 always 應放在一般動詞 works 之前，故 She always works hard. 正確。
5. A — 第一條件句的主要子句用 will + 原形動詞，故選 will。

3. A — backstage 意指「在後台」。
4. C — praise 意指「稱讚」。
