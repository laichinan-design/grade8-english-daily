# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-08

**標題：A Visit to the Doctor**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Mia woke up with a sore throat and a runny nose, so her mother decided to take her to see Dr. Lin.

"Good morning, Mia. What's wrong today?" Dr. Lin asked.

"I have a sore throat, and I feel really tired," Mia said.

"Let me check your throat first," Dr. Lin said. "Hmm, it looks a little red. Do you have a fever?"

"I'm not sure. I feel a bit hot," Mia answered.

Dr. Lin took her temperature. "You have a mild fever. You should drink more water and get enough sleep."

"Should I take medicine?" Mia asked worriedly.

"Yes, I'll give you some medicine. You have to take it three times a day after meals," Dr. Lin explained.

"Can I still go to school tomorrow?" Mia asked.

"If you still feel sick tomorrow, you'd better stay home and rest," Dr. Lin said kindly. "You shouldn't go to school with a fever because you might make your classmates sick, too."

"I understand. Thank you, Dr. Lin," Mia said.

On the way home, Mia's mother bought some warm soup for her. Mia felt a little better after resting all afternoon. The next morning, her fever was gone, so she happily went back to school and told her friends about her visit to the doctor.

## 文法重點

1. **should / shouldn't**：表示建議或不建議做某事，如 *You should drink more water.* / *You shouldn't go to school with a fever.*
2. **have to + 原形動詞**：表示必須，如 *You have to take it three times a day.*
3. **had better (+ not) + 原形動詞**：表示比 should 更強烈的建議，如 *You'd better stay home and rest.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **A** — 文中提到 Mia 喉嚨痛且流鼻水，所以媽媽帶她去看醫生。
2. **B** — 醫生說「Let me check your throat first」。
3. **C** — 醫生說藥要「three times a day after meals」。
4. **B** — 醫生說如果明天還不舒服，最好待在家休息。
5. **C** — 文中提到媽媽買了溫熱的湯給她。

### 文法
1. **A (should)** — should + 原形動詞表示建議。
2. **B (shouldn't)** — 表示「不應該」做某事，用 shouldn't。
3. **A (have to)** — 主詞是 You，have to 表示必須。
4. **A (stay)** — had better 後面接原形動詞。
5. **A (have)** — 疑問句中主詞 you 搭配原形動詞 have。

### 單字
1. **A** — sore throat 指「喉嚨痛」。
2. **A** — fever 指「發燒」。
3. **A** — medicine 指「藥」。
4. **A** — kindly 是「親切地」。
5. **A** — soup 指「湯」，符合句意。
