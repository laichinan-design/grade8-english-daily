## 短文：A Night at the Night Market

On Friday evening, Daniel's family decided to visit the night market near their apartment. The street was crowded with people, and the air was filled with the smell of grilled squid and fried chicken. "I have never seen so many stalls in one place!" Daniel said excitedly to his sister, Amy.

They stopped at a stall that sold bubble tea. The owner, a cheerful man, shouted, "This drink is the most popular one in the whole market!" Daniel decided to try it because he was curious about the taste. Amy chose a cup of mango shaved ice instead, because she thought it looked more refreshing than bubble tea.

As they walked further, they noticed a long line in front of a small shop. "That shop must sell something delicious," their mother guessed. They waited patiently, and soon it was their turn. The stew noodles were too hot to eat right away, so everyone had to wait a few minutes before the noodles were cool enough to eat.

Near the end of the market, Daniel spotted a game stall. He wanted to win a stuffed toy for Amy, so he tried throwing rings at the bottles. Unfortunately, he was not skillful enough, and the rings kept missing. His father, who used to play this game when he was young, offered to help. After several tries, he finally won a small bear for Amy.

Before leaving, they bought a bag of fruit to share at home. "This has been the best night market trip we have had in months," Amy said happily. Daniel agreed that trying new food was always an adventure.

## 文法重點

1. **現在完成時（have/has + 過去分詞）**：表示從過去持續到現在的經驗，或強調剛完成的動作。
   例：*I have never seen so many stalls in one place.* / *This has been the best night market trip we have had in months.*
2. **比較級與最高級**：比較級用於兩者之間的比較，最高級用於三者以上中最突出的一個。
   例：*The shaved ice looked more refreshing than bubble tea.*（比較級）/ *This drink is the most popular one in the whole market.*（最高級）
3. **too...to / 形容詞 + enough + to**：too...to 表示「太...而不能」，enough to 表示「足夠...可以」。
   例：*The noodles were too hot to eat right away.* / *The noodles were cool enough to eat.*

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. Daniel 說這句話是因為他從沒看過這麼多攤位聚在一起，表示他對市場的規模感到驚訝。
2. 文中提到 Amy 選了芒果冰，因為她覺得芒果冰看起來比泡泡茶更清涼提神（more refreshing）。
3. 文中說「The stew noodles were too hot to eat right away」，所以大家必須等麵稍微降溫才能吃。
4. 文中提到 Daniel「was not skillful enough, and the rings kept missing」，所以爸爸主動幫忙。
5. 文末 Amy 說「This has been the best night market trip we have had in months」，表示她非常滿意這次的夜市之旅。

**文法**
1. 現在完成時為 have/has + 過去分詞，see 的過去分詞是 seen，故選 C。
2. 最高級須加 the，且後面常加代名詞 one 代替前面提過的名詞，故選 B。
3. 兩者比較要用比較級 more refreshing than，故選 B。
4. 「too + 形容詞 + to + 動詞」表示「太...而不能...」，故選 too。
5. 「形容詞 + enough + to + 動詞」表示「足夠...可以...」，enough 須放在形容詞之後，故選 C。

**單字**
1. crowded 意思是「擁擠的」，形容人多的地方。
2. stall 指市場或夜市裡的「攤位」。
3. unfortunately 表示「不幸地」，語意接近 sadly（令人遺憾地）。
4. skillful 表示「熟練的」，符合丟圈圈遊戲需要的技巧。
5. adventure 指「冒險」，文中指嘗試新食物的冒險經歷。
