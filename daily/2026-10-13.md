# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-13

**標題：Sending a Package to Grandma**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Amy's grandmother's birthday is tomorrow, so Amy and her brother Tom go to the post office to send her a package.

The clerk weighs the box and says, "Standard mail is cheaper, but it takes five days to arrive." Tom looks worried. "That's too slow! Grandma's birthday is tomorrow."

The clerk smiles. "Express mail is faster. It only takes one day, but it's more expensive than standard mail."

Amy thinks for a moment. "We had better send it by express mail. We don't want to be late."

"Good idea," says Tom. "Is the gift fragile?"

"Yes, it's a glass photo frame," Amy says. "Please pack it carefully."

The clerk checks the address on the package and nods. "Don't worry. We will deliver it in time for her birthday."

Amy and Tom pay for the express mail and leave the post office happily. They know Grandma will be surprised and happy when she opens her gift tomorrow.

## 文法重點

1. **比較級形容詞 (comparative adjectives)**：兩者比較時，形容詞加 -er 或用 more + 形容詞，後接 than，如 *cheaper than*、*more expensive than*。
2. **had better + 原形動詞**：用來給建議或忠告，語氣比 should 更強烈，否定為 had better not，如 *We had better send it by express mail.*
3. **It takes + 時間 + to + 原形動詞**：表示「做某事需要花多少時間」，如 *It takes five days to arrive.*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — 文章開頭提到 Amy 的奶奶生日快到了，所以 Amy 和 Tom 去郵局寄包裹給奶奶。
2. **B** — 店員說「Standard mail is cheaper, but it takes five days to arrive.」，普通郵件比較便宜但要五天。
3. **C** — Tom 說「That's too slow! Grandma's birthday is tomorrow.」，因為奶奶明天生日，普通郵件太慢了。
4. **C** — Amy 說「it's a glass photo frame」，禮物是玻璃相框。
5. **B** — 店員說「We will deliver it in time for her birthday.」，承諾會及時送達。

### 文法
1. **B (more expensive)** — expensive 是多音節形容詞，比較級要用 more + 形容詞 + than。
2. **B (takes)** — 主詞 It 為第三人稱單數，現在時態用 takes，句型為 It takes + 時間 + to V。
3. **A (had better)** — had better 後面直接加原形動詞，不加 to。
4. **B (faster)** — 只比較兩者時用比較級 faster，而非最高級 fastest。
5. **A (be)** — had better 後接原形動詞 be，不可加 to 或變化型。

### 單字
1. **A** — weigh 作動詞意為「稱重」，例如店員用磅秤稱包裹重量。
2. **A (fragile)** — fragile 意為「易碎的」，花瓶易碎需要小心。
3. **B** — in time 意為「及時，趕得上」，例如及時送達給奶奶。
4. **A** — clerk 指「店員、辦事員」，在郵局櫃檯工作的人稱為 clerk。
5. **B** — deliver 作動詞意為「投遞、送達」，例如郵差將包裹送達目的地。
