# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-16

**主題：A Rainy Day Cooking Class（雨天的烹飪課）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

On Wednesday afternoon, it was raining heavily, so Mia and her friend Jack could not have basketball practice outside. Instead, their teacher, Ms. Lin, invited them to join a cooking class in the school kitchen. Although Jack had never cooked before, he decided to try making dumplings for the first time.

Ms. Lin showed the students how to mix flour and water to make the dough. She explained that the dough must rest for twenty minutes before it becomes soft enough to roll out. While waiting, Mia practiced chopping cabbage and carrots into small pieces. She enjoyed cutting the vegetables carefully because she didn't want to hurt her fingers.

When the dough was ready, Jack rolled it into thin circles. At first, his circles were not round at all, but he kept trying and slowly improved. "You might become a great chef someday," Mia said with a smile. Jack laughed and admitted that cooking was harder than he expected.

After they finished filling and folding the dumplings, Ms. Lin steamed them in a large pot. The delicious smell quickly filled the kitchen. Although the dumplings looked a little strange, they tasted amazing. Everyone agreed that Jack's dumplings, though not perfect, were the tastiest ones in the class.

Before leaving, Mia decided to bring some dumplings home for her family. She couldn't wait to tell her parents about the fun rainy afternoon she spent learning to cook.

## 文法重點

1. 動名詞 vs. 不定詞作受詞 (Gerund vs. Infinitive as Object)：enjoy / practice / finish 等動詞後接 V-ing（動名詞）；decide / want / plan 等動詞後接 to + 原形動詞（不定詞）。例如：Mia enjoyed cutting the vegetables carefully.（enjoy + V-ing）；Jack decided to try making dumplings for the first time.（decide + to V）；They finished filling and folding the dumplings.（finish + V-ing）。
2. although 引導讓步子句 (Although + 子句)：表示「雖然…」，用來連接兩個對比的意思，although 子句可放句首或句中，逗號後接主要子句。例如：Although Jack had never cooked before, he decided to try.；Although the dumplings looked a little strange, they tasted amazing.
3. 情態動詞表可能性 (could / might)：could 常表示過去的能力或可能性，用於否定句時表示「（過去）不能」；might 表示現在或未來「或許」的可能性，語氣比 may 更不確定，其後皆接原形動詞。例如：Mia and Jack could not have basketball practice outside.；You might become a great chef someday.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 it was raining heavily, so Mia and her friend Jack could not have basketball practice outside，答案是 B。
2. 文中提到 their teacher, Ms. Lin, invited them to join a cooking class，答案是 C。
3. 文中提到 the dough must rest for twenty minutes before it becomes soft enough to roll out，答案是 C。
4. 文中提到 Mia practiced chopping cabbage and carrots into small pieces，答案是 B。
5. 文末提到 Mia decided to bring some dumplings home for her family，答案是 C。

**文法題**
1. enjoy 後面須接動名詞（V-ing）作受詞，故選 cutting。
2. decide 後面須接不定詞（to + 原形動詞）作受詞，故選 to make。
3. 句意為「雖然…但…」的對比關係，須用 Although 引導讓步子句。
4. 表示過去因下雨而「不能」進行練習，須用 could not（couldn't）。
5. 表示對未來不確定的「或許」可能性，須用情態動詞 might。

**單字題**
1. heavily 意指「猛烈地、大量地」，符合句意「下很大的雨」。
2. dough 意指「麵團」，是麵粉與水混合而成的軟材料。
3. chop 意指「切碎」，符合句意「把食物切成小塊」。
4. admit 意指「承認」，符合句意「即使有點不好意思也說出真話」。
5. steam 意指「蒸」，指用熱蒸氣烹煮食物。
