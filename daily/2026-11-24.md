## 短文：A Family Camping Trip

Last weekend, the Wang family planned to go camping at a lake near the mountains. On Friday night, Dad checked the weather forecast and said, "If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the trip." Luckily, the sky was clear on Saturday morning, so they packed the car and set off early.

When they arrived at the campsite, Mom reminded everyone, "You'd better put on sunscreen before we set up the tent." The tent looked complicated, but with everyone's help, it was ready in twenty minutes. Tim, the youngest, wanted to carry the heaviest cooler by himself, but it was too heavy for him to lift. His sister, Amy, who was strong enough to help, carried it together with him.

In the afternoon, they went hiking along a trail beside the lake. The guide warned that the path was too narrow for two people to walk side by side, so they walked in a single line. They were lucky enough to see a family of ducks swimming near the shore.

At night, they cooked dinner over a campfire and told stories under the stars. Dad said, "If we come back next month, we should bring a bigger tent." Everyone agreed that camping together was more fun than staying at a hotel.

Before they left on Sunday, they made sure to put out the fire completely and clean up all their trash. "If every camper takes care of nature," the park ranger told them, "this lake will stay beautiful for years." The Wang family smiled and promised to return soon.

## 文法重點

1. 第一條件句 (First Conditional)：If + 主詞 + 現在簡單式, 主詞 + will + 動詞原形，用來描述「未來很可能發生」的真實情況。例：If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the trip. / If every camper takes care of nature, this lake will stay beautiful.
2. too...to 與 enough to：too + 形容詞/副詞 + (for sb) + to V 表示「太...以致不能」；形容詞/副詞 + enough + (for sb) + to V 表示「足夠...可以」。兩者語意剛好相反。例：It was too heavy for him to lift. / She was strong enough to help him.
3. had better (+not) + 動詞原形：表示「最好(不要)...」，常用於建議或警告，語氣比 should 更強烈，have/has 可省略為 'd。例：You'd better put on sunscreen before we set up the tent.

## 答案與解析 (Answer Key)

### 閱讀理解

1. **B** — 文中 Dad 說 "If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the trip."，表示若下雨就取消，故選 B。
2. **A** — 文中提到 "it was too heavy for him to lift"，因此他需要姊姊 Amy 的幫忙，故選 A。
3. **C** — 文中寫 "the path was too narrow for two people to walk side by side, so they walked in a single line."，故選 C。
4. **A** — 文中 Dad 說 "If we come back next month, we should bring a bigger tent."，故選 A。
5. **B** — 文中寫 "they made sure to put out the fire completely and clean up all their trash."，故選 B。

### 文法

6. **B** — 第一條件句中 if 子句要用現在簡單式，主詞 it 為第三人稱單數，故動詞加 s，選 B。
7. **C** — 第一條件句的主要子句要用 will + 動詞原形，表示未來的結果，故選 C。
8. **B** — too + 形容詞 + for sb + to V 表示「太...以致某人不能」，故選 B。
9. **C** — 形容詞 + enough + to V 表示「足夠...可以」，enough 要放在形容詞之後，故選 C。
10. **A** — had better + 動詞原形表示「最好...」，不可加 to，故選 A。

### 單字
