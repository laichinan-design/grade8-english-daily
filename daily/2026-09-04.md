# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-04

**主題：Fun at the School Fair（校園園遊會的樂趣）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Every October, our school holds a fair for students and their families. Last Friday, my class helped set up a game booth in the schoolyard. When I was younger, I used to be too shy to talk to customers, but now I really enjoy it.

"Excuse me, how do I play this game?" a boy asked at our booth.

"It's easy for you to try," I said. "Just throw three rings and try to land them on the bottles."

He wasn't as lucky as the girl before him, but he still had fun. My friend Tina ran the food booth next to ours. She used to make cookies with her grandmother every weekend, so she baked dozens of cookies for the fair.

"These cookies are as delicious as the ones at the bakery downtown!" a teacher said happily.

By noon, long lines formed in front of the food booths, and the smell of popcorn filled the air. It was hard for us to find a place to sit, so we shared a table with another class.

In the afternoon, our booth earned enough money to buy new books for the library. "I used to think fairs were just for fun," our teacher said, "but they can also teach us about teamwork and hard work."

At the end of the day, we were tired but proud. It wasn't as hard as we expected, and everyone agreed the fair was a great success.

## 文法重點

1. **used to + 原形動詞**：表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已不再如此。例如：*I used to be too shy to talk to customers, but now I really enjoy it.*（used to be 表示過去害羞、現在不同）；*Tina used to make cookies with her grandmother every weekend.*
2. **as + 原級(形容詞/副詞) + as**：表示「跟…一樣」，否定 not as...as 表示「不如…」。例如：*These cookies are as delicious as the ones at the bakery downtown.*（同等比較）；*He wasn't as lucky as the girl before him.*（否定同等比較，表示「不如」）。
3. **It's/It was + 形容詞 + for + 某人 + to V**：表示「對某人而言做某事是…的」，不定詞片語作真正主詞的常見句型。例如：*It's easy for you to try.*；*It was hard for us to find a place to sit.*
