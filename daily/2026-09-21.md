# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-21

**主題：A School Sports Day（學校運動會）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Every autumn, Wenshan Junior High holds a sports day, and this year Mia could not wait to compete. She had practiced running every morning before school, hoping to become the fastest runner in her class.

When the sports day began, the whole school gathered on the field. Teachers announced each event through a loudspeaker, and students cheered loudly for their teammates. "You can do it! Don't give up!" Mia's friends shouted as she lined up for the 100-meter race.

The race was one of the most exciting events of the day. While Mia was running, she suddenly heard her name being called from the crowd. That gave her the courage to run even faster. She crossed the finish line first and won a gold medal.

Later, Mia's class took part in the tug-of-war. It was tough, but everyone pulled together as a team. Unfortunately, they lost to the class next door. "It's okay," said their teacher. "Losing a game doesn't mean you failed. What matters is that you tried your best and encouraged each other."

By the end of the day, Mia was exhausted but proud. She had won a medal, made new memories with her teammates, and learned that teamwork is just as important as victory. As she walked home, she was already looking forward to next year's sports day.

## 文法重點

1. 最高級 the + 形容詞-est/most + 名詞：表示在某個範圍中「最...的」，用來比較三者(含)以上。例如：Mia hoped to become the fastest runner in her class.；It was one of the toughest games of the day.
2. one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞：表示「...之一」，強調某事物在同類中名列前茅，後面一定接複數名詞。例如：The race was one of the most exciting events of the day.；Tug-of-war is one of the most popular games at school sports days.
3. when/while + 過去進行式，主要子句用過去簡單式：表示「正當...的時候，另一件事發生了」，過去進行式描述背景中正在進行的動作。例如：While Mia was running, she suddenly heard her name being called from the crowd.；While the students were practicing hard, the crowd cheered loudly for them.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 hoping to become the fastest runner in her class，她練跑是希望成為班上跑最快的人，答案是 B。
2. 文中提到 Teachers announced each event through a loudspeaker，答案是 B。
3. 文中提到 While Mia was running, she suddenly heard her name being called from the crowd，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 Unfortunately, they lost to the class next door，答案是 C。
5. 文中提到 she learned that teamwork is just as important as victory，答案是 B。

**文法題**
1. 表示「班上最快的」要用最高級 the fastest，故選 C。
2. one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞，表示「最...的其中之一」，故選 the most exciting。
3. 表示「正當...的時候」要用 While 加過去進行式，故選 While。
4. while 子句表示背景中正在進行的動作，要用過去進行式 were practicing，故選 C。
5. one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞的固定用法，須加 the，故選 the most popular。

**單字題**
1. compete 指「比賽、競爭」，文中指 Mia 想參加運動會的比賽。
2. courage 指「勇氣」，意思最接近 bravery。
3. 句意指 Mia 到了一天結束時感到「精疲力盡」，應選 exhausted。
4. teamwork 指「團隊合作」。
5. tough 指「艱難的、強悍的」，文中形容拔河比賽很艱難。
