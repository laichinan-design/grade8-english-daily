# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-23

**主題：A Busy Morning at the Breakfast Shop（早餐店的忙碌早晨）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Every weekday morning, Mr. Chen opens his small breakfast shop at six o'clock. He always arrives early to prepare fresh vegetables and eggs before the first customers come in. His shop is famous for its hot soy milk and crispy sandwiches.

Today, a young student named Tina hurried into the shop. "Good morning! Can I have a ham and egg sandwich and a cup of hot soy milk, please?" she asked. "Sure," Mr. Chen said with a smile. "It usually takes about five minutes to make a sandwich, so please wait a little while."

While she was waiting, Tina checked her watch nervously. Her bus would leave in ten minutes, and she still needed to walk to the bus stop. "Don't worry," Mr. Chen said. "I'll make it as fast as I can." Before she finished paying, the sandwich was already wrapped and ready.

Tina often comes to this shop before school because the food is delicious and not expensive. She usually orders the same sandwich, but sometimes she tries something new, like scrambled eggs. After she said thank you, she ran out of the shop and caught the bus just in time.

Mr. Chen never complains about the busy mornings. "Seeing happy customers makes it all worth it," he always says. By nine o'clock, most of the morning rush is over, and he can finally take a short break before cleaning up for the day.

## 文法重點

1. 頻率副詞 (Adverbs of Frequency: always, usually, often, sometimes, never)：置於一般動詞之前、be動詞/助動詞之後，表示動作發生的頻率。例如：He always arrives early to prepare fresh vegetables.；Mr. Chen never complains about the busy mornings.
2. Before / After 連接詞：連接兩個子句，表示動作發生的先後順序，前後子句動詞時態需一致。例如：Before she finished paying, the sandwich was already wrapped.；After she said thank you, she ran out of the shop.
3. It takes + (人) + 時間 + to V：表示做某件事所需要花費的時間。例如：It usually takes about five minutes to make a sandwich.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 Mr. Chen opens his small breakfast shop at six o'clock，答案是 B。
2. 文中 Tina 說 Can I have a ham and egg sandwich and a cup of hot soy milk, please?，答案是 C。
3. 文中提到 Her bus would leave in ten minutes，所以她緊張地看錶，答案是 A。
4. 文中提到 the food is delicious and not expensive，答案是 D。
5. 文中提到 he can finally take a short break before cleaning up for the day，答案是 C。

**文法題**
1. 頻率副詞 always 放在一般動詞 arrives 之前，故選 A。
2. 句意為「她付完錢之前，三明治就已經包好了」，故選 C（Before）。
3. It takes + 時間 + to V 句型，主詞 It 為第三人稱單數，動詞用 takes，故選 D。
4. 句意為「她道謝之後才跑出店外」，故選 C（After）。
5. 頻率副詞 never 表示「從不」，放在一般動詞 complains 之前，故選 B。

**單字題**
1. customer 指「顧客」。
2. delicious 指「美味的」，意思最接近 tasty。
3. 句意指麵包「酥脆」，應選 crispy。
4. nervously 指「緊張地」。
5. complain 指「抱怨」。
