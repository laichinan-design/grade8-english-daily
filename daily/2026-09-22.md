# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-22

**主題：A Visit to the Dentist（看牙醫記）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Last week, Amy had a terrible toothache. It was so painful that she could not eat her favorite ice cream. Her mother said, "You have to see a dentist right away." Amy felt nervous because she used to be afraid of the dentist's office when she was younger.

When they arrived, the dentist smiled and said, "Don't worry, the checkup is painless." First, the dentist took an X-ray to look inside Amy's tooth. She found a small cavity and had to fill it carefully. Amy closed her eyes, but the treatment was over before she knew it.

"You must brush your teeth twice a day and floss every night," the dentist explained. "You should also eat less sugar and drink more water." Amy nodded and promised to build healthier habits.

On the way home, Amy felt so relieved that she wanted to celebrate. "I used to hate going to the dentist," she told her mother, "but now I know it helps me stay healthy." From that day on, Amy made a dentist appointment every six months and never had a serious toothache again.

## 文法重點

1. so + 形容詞/副詞 + that + 結果子句：表示「如此...以致於...」，強調原因造成的結果。例如：It was so painful that she could not eat her favorite ice cream.；Amy felt so relieved that she wanted to celebrate.
2. have to / must 表示義務「必須」：have to 常用於客觀、一般性的必要；must 常用於說話者主觀強調或重要規定。例如：You have to see a dentist right away.；You must brush your teeth twice a day.
3. used to + 原形動詞：表示「過去經常/曾經」的習慣或狀態，暗示現在已不再如此。例如：Amy used to be afraid of the dentist's office when she was younger.；I used to hate going to the dentist.

### 中文解析

**閱讀理解**
1. 文中提到 It was so painful that she could not eat her favorite ice cream，因為牙痛太痛了，答案是 B。
2. 文中提到 Amy used to be afraid of the dentist's office when she was younger，答案是 B。
3. 文中提到 First, the dentist took an X-ray to look inside Amy's tooth，答案是 B。
4. 文中提到 dentist 要求 Amy 每晚 floss every night，答案是 C。
5. 文中提到 Amy felt so relieved that she wanted to celebrate，答案是 C。

**文法題**
1. so + 形容詞 + that 子句表示「如此...以致於...」，故選 A。
2. 表示「必須」的義務，用 have to，故選 A。
3. used to + 原形動詞表示「過去經常/曾經」的習慣，故選 B。
4. must 表示強調的義務或規定，故選 A。
5. so...that... 為固定搭配，表示結果，故選 A。

**單字題**
1. toothache 指「牙痛」，文中指 Amy 上週牙痛。
2. nervous 指「緊張的」，意思最接近 anxious。
3. 句意指健康檢查「不痛」，應選 painless。
4. cavity 指「蛀牙」。
5. relieved 指「鬆一口氣的」，文中形容 Amy 看完牙醫後放心的感覺。
