# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-10-06

**標題：Joining the Basketball Club**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Ben saw a poster about a new basketball club on the school bulletin board. He had always wanted to join a sports club, so he decided to ask Mr. Lin, the P.E. teacher, for more information.

"Excuse me, Mr. Lin. I'd like to join the basketball club. Could you tell me more about it?" Ben asked.

"Sure," Mr. Lin said. "We practice every Tuesday and Thursday, right after school. The practice is more exciting than you might think, and you'll get stronger if you keep practicing!"

Ben laughed. "That sounds great. Is it difficult for beginners?"

"Not at all," Mr. Lin replied. "Many students join without any experience. Before you start, you just need to bring comfortable shoes and a water bottle. After each practice, we clean up the gym together."

"What if I'm busy on Tuesdays?" Ben asked, a little worried.

"Don't worry. You can come on Thursday first and decide later," Mr. Lin said kindly. "The basketball club is friendlier than most people think, and everyone helps each other."

Ben felt relieved and signed his name immediately. He wanted to improve his skills and make new friends.

That afternoon, Ben told his best friend, Alex, about the club. "Why don't you join with me?" Ben asked. "It will be more fun if we practice together."

Alex smiled and said, "I'd love to! Basketball is more interesting than studying, isn't it?"

They both laughed and walked home, excited for their first practice next Tuesday.

## 文法重點

1. **I'd like to（= I would like to）**：表示委婉的「我想要」，比 want to 更禮貌，常用於請求或表達願望，如 *I'd like to join the basketball club.*
2. **形容詞比較級 (Comparative Adjectives)**：兩者比較時，單音節字尾加 -er（*stronger*），多音節字則在前面加 more（*more exciting*），以子音+y 結尾的字去 y 加 -ier（*friendly → friendlier*），後面常接 than。
3. **before / after 引導的時間子句**：before 表示「在...之前」，after 表示「在...之後」，用來說明動作發生的先後順序，如 *Before you start, you need...* / *After each practice, we clean up...*

## 答案與解析 Key

### 閱讀理解
1. **B** — 文中提到 Ben 想加入籌球社，所以去找 Mr. Lin 詢問更多資訊。
2. **B** — Mr. Lin 說「We practice every Tuesday and Thursday」。
3. **B** — 文中提到新成員需要帶「comfortable shoes and a water bottle」。
4. **C** — 文中寫道「Ben felt relieved and signed his name immediately」，表示他感到放心。
5. **A** — Ben 問 Alex「Why don't you join with me?」，邀請他一起加入籌球社。

### 文法
1. **A (would like)** — would like to 表示委婉的「想要」，比 want to 更禮貌。
2. **C (more exciting)** — 兩者比較需用比較級，多音節形容詞前加 more，且後面接 than。
3. **B (friendlier)** — friendly 字尾是子音+y，比較級要去 y 加 -ier。
4. **B (Before)** — 表示「開始之前」要用 before 引導時間子句。
5. **B (After)** — 表示「每次練習之後」要用 after。

### 單字
1. **A** — poster 指貼在牆上或公告欄的「海報」。
2. **B** — beginner 指剛開始學習、沒有經驗的人，即「初學者」。
3. **B** — relieved 表示原本擔心的事解決後感到「放心的」。
4. **A** — bulletin board 是學校或社區張貼公告的「公告欄」。
5. **A** — want to 之後要接動詞原形，故選 improve。
