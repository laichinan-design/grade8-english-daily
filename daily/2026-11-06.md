# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-06

**標題：** A Busy Day at the Animal Shelter
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

On Saturday morning, Lily and her brother Jason went to the city animal shelter to volunteer. "This is my first time," Lily said nervously. "I hope I can do a good job." A kind worker named Mr. Chen smiled and said, "Don't worry. Just be patient and gentle with the animals."

Their first job was to feed the kittens. Jason thought feeding kittens would be easier than cleaning cages, but he was wrong. One small kitten was so shy that it hid under a blanket and would not come out. Lily spoke softly and waited quietly until the kitten finally walked over to eat.

Next, they helped walk the dogs. A big brown dog named Max pulled hard on his leash and barked loudly at every car. "Max is louder than all the other dogs here," Jason laughed. Mr. Chen explained that Max used to live on the street, so he was more nervous than the other dogs.

By the afternoon, Lily and Jason had learned a lot. They discovered that caring for animals takes more patience than they expected. The most difficult animal to handle was Max, but he also became the friendliest by the end of the day. When it was time to go home, Lily felt grateful. "I have never felt so proud of myself," she said. Jason agreed, "Let's come back next weekend. Maybe we can even help find a family for Max."

## 📚 文法重點

1. **比較級 (comparative, -er than / more ... than)**：用來比較兩者，形容詞字尾加 -er 或前面加 more，後接 than，表示「比...更...」。
   例：*Feeding kittens would be easier than cleaning cages.* / *Caring for animals takes more patience than they expected.*
2. **最高級 (superlative, the -est / the most ...)**：用來比較三者以上，形容詞字尾加 -est 或前面加 the most，表示「最...的」。
   例：*The most difficult animal to handle was Max.* / *He also became the friendliest by the end of the day.*
3. **used to + 動詞原形**：表示過去的習慣或狀態，而現在已經不是如此，強調「以前常常」或「過去曾經」。
   例：*Max used to live on the street, so he was more nervous than the other dogs.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到「This is my first time,」Lily said nervously，表示這是她第一次當志工，所以感到緊張。
2. **C** — 文中提到「Their first job was to feed the kittens」，他們的第一個工作是餵小貓。
3. **B** — 文中提到「One small kitten was so shy that it hid under a blanket」，因為牠很害羞怕生。
4. **C** — 文中提到「Max used to live on the street, so he was more nervous than the other dogs」。
5. **C** — 文中提到 Lily felt grateful 並說「I have never felt so proud of myself」，表示她感到感激又自豪。
6. **B** — 比較兩者用比較級，easy 字尾加 -er 變成 easier，後接 than，故選 easier。
7. **B** — 與其他狗比較用比較級 louder than，loud 為單音節形容詞字尾直接加 -er。
8. **C** — 與全部動物比較（三者以上）要用最高級 the most difficult，difficult 為多音節形容詞前面加 the most。
9. **B** — used to + 動詞原形表示過去的習慣或狀態，現在已不是如此，故選 used to。
10. **A** — patience 為不可數名詞，比較級用 more...than，故選 more。
11. **B** — volunteer 意為「自願、當志工」，指不求報酬主動幫忙。
12. **C** — shelter 指「收容所」，照顧走失或受傷的動物。
13. **B** — nervous 意為「緊張的」，指感到擔心或有點害怕。
14. **B** — gentle 意為「溫和的」，對待他人輕柔和善。
15. **B** — grateful 意為「感激的」，表示對某事心懷感謝。
