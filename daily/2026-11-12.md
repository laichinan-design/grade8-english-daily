# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-12

**標題：** Ben Calls to Book a Table for Dinner
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Ben's family wanted to celebrate his sister's graduation, so his father asked him to call a new Italian restaurant downtown and make a reservation. Ben had never called a restaurant before, so he felt a little nervous.

"Good evening, Luigi's Kitchen. How can I help you?" a friendly voice answered.

"Hi, I'd like to book a table for five people this Saturday evening, please," Ben said politely.

"Of course. Could you tell me what time you would prefer?" the waiter asked.

Ben checked with his father and answered, "Around seven o'clock, if that's possible."

"Let me check... Yes, we have a table available at seven. May I have your name and phone number?" the waiter asked.

After Ben gave the information, the waiter said, "Great! We'll reserve a table near the window for you. Please arrive ten minutes early, since the restaurant gets busy on weekends."

Ben thanked him and hung up the phone, feeling proud of himself. He told his father that everything was arranged and that the restaurant was large enough to hold their whole family comfortably.

On Saturday, the family arrived exactly on time. The waiter recognized Ben's name immediately and led them to their reserved table. His sister was surprised and asked how he had managed to plan everything so smoothly.

"It wasn't hard," Ben smiled. "I just asked politely and listened carefully to what the waiter told me to do."

That evening, the family enjoyed a wonderful dinner together, and Ben realized that making a simple phone call could make such a big difference.

## 📚 文法重點

1. **間接問句 (Embedded Questions)**：當疑問句放入另一個句子中作受詞時，語序要改成「主詞+動詞」的正常敘述句語序，不能用疑問句語序，也不加 do/does/did。例：Could you tell me what time you would prefer?（不是 what time would you prefer）/ She asked how he had managed to plan everything so smoothly.
2. **ask/tell + 受詞 + to V（受詞補語用法）**：ask/tell 後面先接「人」，再接 to + 動詞原形，表示「要求/告訴某人做某事」。例：His father asked him to call a new Italian restaurant. / The waiter told Ben to arrive ten minutes early.
3. **形容詞/副詞 + enough (+ to V)**：enough 要放在形容詞或副詞「之後」，表示「足夠...（可以...）」。例：The restaurant was large enough to hold their whole family comfortably.

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到爸爸要 Ben 打電話去一家新的義大利餐廳預約，為了慶祝姊姊畢業。
2. **B** — Ben 回答服務生「Around seven o'clock」，所以是七點。
3. **B** — 服務生說 the restaurant gets busy on weekends，所以請他們提早十分鐘到。
4. **B** — 文中提到姊姊很驚訝，問他是怎麼把一切安排得那麼順利的。
5. **C** — 文末提到 Ben realized that making a simple phone call could make such a big difference。
6. **B** — 間接問句要用「主詞+動詞」的正常語序，故選 you would prefer，不能用疑問句語序 would you prefer。
7. **C** — 間接問句中動詞要用正常敘述句語序與適當時態，文中用過去完成式 had managed。
8. **C** — ask + 受詞 + to V，表示「要求某人做某事」，故選 to call。
9. **C** — tell + 受詞 + to V，表示「告訴某人做某事」，故選 to arrive。
10. **A** — 形容詞 + enough + to V，表示「足夠...可以...」，故選 enough to。
11. **B** — nervous 意為「緊張的」，指對某事感到擔心或不安。
12. **C** — available 意為「可用的、有空的」，指還沒被預訂、可以使用。
13. **B** — reserve 意為「預留」，指先保留某物以供之後使用。
14. **B** — recognize 意為「認出」，指因為先前見過而知道這是誰。
15. **A** — arrange 意為「安排」，指仔細規劃或組織某事。
