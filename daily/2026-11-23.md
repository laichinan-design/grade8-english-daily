# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-23

**程度**：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)
**標題**：A Saturday at the Animal Shelter

## 短文 Passage

Last Saturday, Mia and her brother Jack decided to volunteer at the city animal shelter. Their teacher had told them that the shelter needed more helpers on weekends, so they signed up right away.

When they arrived, a staff member named Mr. Chen greeted them warmly. He said that most of the animals were strays that people had found on the street. Some of the dogs looked nervous, but others wagged their tails happily when Mia walked past their cages.

Mia's first job was to feed the cats. She had to be gentle so that the shy ones would not run away. Jack's job was harder: he had to walk the dogs on a leash, one by one. The biggest dog, a brown Labrador named Rocky, pulled so hard that Jack almost fell down!

During lunch, Mr. Chen told the volunteers that the shelter was looking for people to adopt the animals. He explained that a cat's care is almost as expensive as a dog's, so families should think carefully before adopting.

By the end of the day, Mia and Jack were exhausted, but they felt proud. Jack said that he wanted to come back every weekend. Mia asked, "Can we adopt a kitten?" She wanted to give one of the cats a real home.

## 文法重點 Grammar Points

1. 間接引述－直述句 (Reported Speech)：把別人說的話轉成間接句時，若原句是現在簡單式，轉述時動詞要「時態倒退」一個時態(現在→過去)，常用 said/told (sb) that + 子句。例：Mr. Chen said, "The shelter needs more helpers." → Mr. Chen said that the shelter needed more helpers. / Jack said that he wanted to come back every weekend.
2. 間接疑問句 (Reported Questions)：轉述問句時，語序改為「主詞+動詞」的敘述句語序，助動詞也要時態倒退(can→could, will→would)，句尾不加問號。例：Mia asked, "Can we adopt a kitten?" → Mia asked if they could adopt a kitten.
3. so that 表「目的」 + as...as 表「同等比較」：so that + 子句表示「為了使...」；as + 形容詞/副詞 + as 表示「跟...一樣」。例：She was gentle so that the shy cats would not run away. / A cat's care is almost as expensive as a dog's.

## 答案與解析 Answer Key

1. B — 文中提到 "Their teacher had told them that the shelter needed more helpers on weekends, so they signed up right away."，故選 B。
2. B — 文中寫 "Mia's first job was to feed the cats."，故選 B。
3. B — 文中提到 "The biggest dog... pulled so hard that Jack almost fell down!"，故選 B。
4. B — 文中寫 "a cat's care is almost as expensive as a dog's, so families should think carefully before adopting."，故選 B。
5. C — 文中寫 "Mia asked, 'Can we adopt a kitten?'"，故選 C。
6. B — 轉述直述句時，現在簡單式要倒退為過去簡單式，needs → needed，故選 B。
7. B — so that + 子句表示「為了使...」，說明動作的目的，故選 B。
8. B — 表示「跟...一樣」要用 as + 形容詞 + as 的結構，故選 B。
9. B — 轉述時主詞動詞 said 用過去式，且 wants→wanted 要時態倒退，故選 B。
10. B — 轉述問句時，助動詞 can 也要時態倒退為 could，故選 B。
11. B — stray 指「走失的、無家可歸的(動物)」，故選 B。
12. A — adopt 意為「領養」，符合句意，故選 A。
13. B — exhausted 意為「疲憊不堪的」，故選 B。
14. A — wag 指狗尾巴「搖動」，故選 A。
15. B — shelter 意為「收容所、庇護所」，故選 B。
