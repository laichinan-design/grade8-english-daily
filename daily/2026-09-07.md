# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-09-07

**主題：A Surprise Birthday Party（驚喜生日派對）**
**程度：國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)**

## 短文

Next Saturday is Mia's birthday, so her friends are going to plan a surprise party for her. "Let's decorate the classroom after school," said Ben. "We can hang balloons and put a big banner on the wall."

"That's a great idea," said Lily. "I'm going to invite ten guests, but we have to keep it a secret. If Mia finds out, the surprise will be ruined."

The plan was so exciting that everyone wanted to help. Some students bought a gift, and others made a card. Ben was going to bring a cake from his mother's bakery.

On Friday afternoon, the classroom was so quiet that Mia felt curious. "Why is everyone whispering?" she thought. She had no idea what was waiting for her.

When Mia opened the door, her classmates shouted, "Surprise!" Balloons filled the room, and the banner said, "Happy Birthday, Mia!" She was so surprised that she almost cried.

"We wanted to celebrate your special day," Lily said. "We hope you like it."

"I love it," Mia said. "This is the best birthday ever! Thank you all for keeping the secret."

Everyone sang and ate cake together. Ben's mother's cake was so delicious that people asked for more. Before Mia left, she said, "Let's take a photo to remember this day."

It was a wonderful afternoon, and Mia felt lucky to have such caring friends.

## 文法重點

1. **be going to + 原形動詞**：表示已計畫好的未來打算。例如：*Her friends are going to plan a surprise party.*（未來計畫）；*Ben was going to bring a cake from his mother's bakery.*（過去式 was going to 表示「當時打算」）。
2. **so + 形容詞/副詞 + that + 子句**：表示「如此…以致於…」的結果。例如：*The plan was so exciting that everyone wanted to help.*；*She was so surprised that she almost cried.*
3. **Let's + 原形動詞**：用來提出建議，表示「我們一起…吧」。例如：*Let's decorate the classroom after school.*；*Let's take a photo to remember this day.*

## 答案 Key 與解析

**閱讀理解**
1. B — 文中提到「Next Saturday is Mia's birthday」。
2. C — 文中提到「Ben was going to bring a cake from his mother's bakery」。
3. A — 文中提到「the classroom was so quiet that Mia felt curious」。
4. C — 文中提到「the banner said, "Happy Birthday, Mia!"」。
5. B — 文中提到「Let's take a photo to remember this day」。

**文法**
1. A — be going to + 原形動詞表示已計畫好的未來打算，故用 is going to。
2. C — so + 形容詞 + that + 子句表示「如此…以致於…」。
3. A — Let's + 原形動詞用來提出建議，Let's 為 Let us 的縮寫。
4. B — 描述過去計畫用 was going to，故用過去式 was。
5. C — so + 形容詞 + that + 子句的固定句型，空格應填 that。

**單字**
1. B — secret 意指「祕密的；祕密」。
2. A — decorate 意指「布置；裝飾」。
3. C — guest 意指「客人；賓客」。
4. A — ruin 意指「毀壞；破壞」。
5. B — celebrate 意指「慶祝」。
