# 國中英文每日任務 — 2026-11-09

**標題：** Mia Gets Lost at the Train Station
**適用年級：** 國中二~三年級 (Grade 8-9)

## 📖 短文

Last Saturday, Mia and her mom went to the train station to visit her aunt in Taichung. The station was very crowded, and people were rushing in every direction. While her mom was buying tickets, Mia walked over to look at a big map on the wall. When she turned around a few minutes later, her mom was gone.

Mia felt confused and scared. The station was too big for her to find her way alone. A loud announcement came on over the speaker, but it was so noisy that she could not understand a single word. She stood near the map and tried to stay calm, just like her mom had taught her.

A kind station worker who was wearing a blue uniform noticed Mia standing alone. "Are you lost, little girl?" he asked gently. Mia nodded and explained that she could not find her mother. The worker, whose name tag said Mr. Lin, walked Mia to the information desk and made an announcement over the speaker.

A few minutes later, Mia's mom came running, out of breath. "I was so worried that I couldn't think straight!" she said, hugging Mia tightly. Mia promised that she would never wander off again. Before they left, Mia thanked Mr. Lin, who smiled and said, "That's exactly what a good traveler does — stay calm and ask for help."

## 📚 文法重點

1. **關係代詞 who / whose（修飾人的關係子句）**：who 代替「人」作主詞，whose 代替「人的所有格」，用來補充說明前面提到的人。
   例：*A kind station worker who was wearing a blue uniform noticed Mia.* / *The worker, whose name tag said Mr. Lin, walked Mia to the information desk.*
2. **too + 形容詞 + to + 動詞原形**：表示「太...而不能...」，後面的動作因為程度太過而無法完成。
   例：*The station was too big for her to find her way alone.*
3. **so + 形容詞 + that + 子句**：表示「如此...以致於...」，強調程度深到造成某個結果。
   例：*It was so noisy that she could not understand a single word.* / *I was so worried that I couldn't think straight.*

## ✅ 答案 Key 與解析

1. **B** — 文中提到 Mia and her mom went to the train station to visit her aunt in Taichung，她們是去搭車拜訪阿姨。
2. **B** — 文中提到 Mia walked over to look at a big map on the wall，她當時正在看牆上的地圖。
3. **B** — 文中提到 it was so noisy that she could not understand a single word，因為太吵聽不清楚。
4. **B** — 文中提到 the worker walked Mia to the information desk and made an announcement over the speaker。
5. **B** — 文中結尾提到 that's exactly what a good traveler does — stay calm and ask for help，即遇到困難要保持冷靜並尋求協助。
6. **B** — worker 是「人」，作關係子句的主詞，故用關係代詞 who。
7. **B** — too + 形容詞 + to + 動詞原形，表示「太...而不能...」，故選 too。
8. **C** — so + 形容詞 + that + 子句，表示「如此...以致於...」，故選 so。
9. **B** — 後面接 that 子句表示結果，須用 so + 形容詞 + that，故選 so。
10. **C** — 表示「他的」名牌，為所有格關係代詞，故選 whose。
11. **B** — crowded 意為「擁擠的」，指某處有很多人。
12. **B** — confused 意為「困惑的」，指不了解正在發生的事。
13. **B** — announcement 意為「廣播、公告」，指向大眾傳達的訊息。
14. **C** — out of breath 意為「喘不過氣」，指因跑步等而呼吸急促。
15. **B** — promise 意為「承諾」，指堅定地表示會做某事。
